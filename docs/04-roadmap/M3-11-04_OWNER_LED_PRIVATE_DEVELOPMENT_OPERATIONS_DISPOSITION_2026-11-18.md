# M3-11-04 — Owner-Led Private Development Operations Disposition

**Date:** 2026-11-18  
**Status:** INTERNAL DEVELOPMENT BOUNDARY RECORDED — HOSTED OPERATIONS READINESS DEFERRED  
**Package:** M3-11-04 Human Operations and Support Readiness

## 1. Why the original nine-role register is not a current development prerequisite

The original M3-11-04 preparation pack defines nine accountable roles for a future operated acceptance/release context. That is a useful future checklist, but it should not be interpreted as a requirement to recruit or appoint nine people before continuing repository-only private V2 development.

The current authorized activity is private development and internal acceptance using synthetic/fictional fixtures only. No hosted acceptance environment is provisioned or authorized; no real users, students, institutions, providers or personal records are involved; no public support intake, participant activity, pilot, deployment or release is authorized.

For this bounded activity, the Product Owner remains the sole accountable human for scope, internal decisions and acceptance. The assistant may prepare code, tests, analysis and documentation but is not an operator, independent reviewer, incident commander, privacy officer, safeguarding lead or release approver.

## 2. Current owner-led operating boundary

| Responsibility | Current handling | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Product scope and internal acceptance | Product Owner | Owner decisions are limited to private synthetic V2 work |
| Repository implementation and test preparation | AI-assisted development under owner direction | AI cannot self-certify or approve its own work |
| Code review of AI-produced work | Product Owner internal review, supported by CI and documented evidence | Not independent assurance |
| Defect/issue tracking | Repository issues/PRs using synthetic examples only | No public support intake or real-user reports |
| Privacy/security/safeguarding escalation | Stop work and refer to Product Owner; no real data or participants in current scope | Not a staffed incident-response service |
| Release/change execution | Not applicable; no release/deployment authorized | Separate authorization required |
| Backup/restore/recovery operations | Not applicable; no hosted environment or data store authorized | M3-11-05/06 gates remain blocked |

## 3. Explicit deferrals

The following roles and operational mechanisms are deferred until a future activity actually requires them:

- V2 Operations Lead and deputy
- Incident Commander and on-call coverage
- Support Lead and service-level response commitments
- Privacy/Data Protection and Safeguarding leads
- Security operations lead
- Release/change operator
- Recovery owner and independent recovery verifier

Before any hosted private acceptance environment, external reviewer access, real user/participant activity, public support intake, pilot or release is proposed, the Product Owner must revisit the role matrix, identify the exact required responsibilities, decide which roles may be combined, and obtain written acceptance/competence/availability evidence appropriate to that activity. This is a future gate, not a requirement to recruit now.

## 4. Stop conditions retained

Stop the current activity if any real personal/student/guardian data, public participant, hosted environment, production/V1 resource, external service, unapproved spend, deployment, incident requiring real-world response, or scope expansion appears. Do not treat the absence of a staffed operations team as permission to proceed into any such activity.

## 5. Disposition

- Repository-only private development: **OWNER-LED MODEL ACCEPTED FOR CURRENT BOUNDARY**.
- Nine-role operational roster: **DEFERRED; 0/9 acceptances remains factually unchanged**.
- Hosted private acceptance operations: **NOT READY / NOT AUTHORIZED**.
- Public support, pilot, production and release operations: **NOT AUTHORIZED**.
- M3-11-04: **CLOSED FOR CURRENT REPOSITORY-ONLY INTERNAL DEVELOPMENT SCOPE; FUTURE HOSTED/RELEASE OPERATIONS GATE REMAINS OPEN**.

This disposition narrows the applicability of the role register; it does not claim that operational readiness exists or waive future staffing, recovery, incident or release requirements.


## Product Owner amendment — 2026-11-25: two-superadmin model

The current product administration model is defined as **the Product Owner plus a maximum of two Superadmins (administrative accounts)**. The Product Owner is the owner; two Superadmin accounts are the current planned administrator capacity. The system may later allow controlled expansion or configuration of additional administrative accounts with permission limitations, but that extension is not required now. All other accounts are end users and must not receive administrative privileges by default. No broader operational roster, staffing matrix, deputies, support team, incident commander, privacy lead, security lead, safeguarding lead, release operator or recovery operator is required as a product-development agenda item; if an operational mechanism becomes necessary for a future activity, it will be designed and authorized at that time. This product-owner decision does not itself implement or verify the role/permission enforcement, appoint real people, authorize hosted/live operation, or waive applicable safeguards.

**Disposition updated:** Current development/admin model: Owner + up to 2 Superadmins; other accounts end users. Future operational staffing: not a current project requirement; build/reconcile only if a future activity requires it.
