## 1. Purpose

This document records a practical two-person administrative arrangement for continuing safe M3-10 readiness preparation without hiring a team or creating new costs. It does not reduce or replace any required qualified independent review.

## 2. Proposed two-person arrangement

| Person | Proposed role | Permitted responsibilities |
|---|---|---|
| Product Owner (user) | Product Owner / Project Administrator | Set project priorities; decide and approve exact project scope; review progress; approve documentation; decide whether to seek qualified reviewers; retain final project decisions and stop authority. |
| Second person (ordinary/general experience) | Administrative Assistant / Pilot Coordination Assistant | Organize approved documents; maintain a task and decision log; record questions and defects reported during permitted synthetic exercises; check that checklist fields are completed; coordinate document handoffs when the Product Owner directs; escalate uncertainty or safety concerns to the Product Owner. |

The second person's role is administrative. It is not a technical administrator, developer, security assessor, legal/privacy reviewer, safeguarding specialist, accessibility auditor, incident responder or independent approver. Do not assign those duties based on availability or job title alone.

No person's identity, acceptance, availability, qualifications or commitment is recorded here. The Product Owner must confirm any appointment directly with that person before treating the role as active.
