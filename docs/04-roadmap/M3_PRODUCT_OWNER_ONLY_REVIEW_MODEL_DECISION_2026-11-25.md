# M3 Product Owner Review Model — Decision and Roadmap Reconciliation — 2026-11-25

**Repository:** `mattari20/listing-v2`
**Decision owner:** Product Owner
**Status:** APPROVED — owner-only review model
**Effective date:** 2026-11-25

## 1. Product Owner decision

The Product Owner has expressly decided that M3 does **not require second-party, independent, external, professional, or paid review** as a project acceptance requirement. M3 review and acceptance will be performed solely by the Product Owner.

This decision supersedes prior roadmap and gate wording that made independent legal/privacy/safeguarding, security, or accessibility reviewers mandatory prerequisites to Product Owner acceptance or M3 completion. The independent-reviewer selection/engagement agenda is cancelled and will not be pursued.

## 2. Owner-led review method

The Product Owner will review project evidence, documentation, synthetic test results, risks, limitations, and acceptance exercises, with ChatGPT assisting by organizing evidence, explaining issues, preparing checklists/remediation plans, and recording decisions.

- The Product Owner is the sole acceptance authority.
- ChatGPT assistance is not a second-party review and does not represent an independent assessor.
- No external reviewer recruitment, engagement, procurement, fee, or review is required.
- No independent-assurance claim or certification will be made.
- Professional or external assurance, if ever considered, is optional—not a blocker, requirement, or implied authorization.

## 3. Gate and blocker reconciliation

The independent-review requirement is removed from M3 acceptance criteria. The corresponding readiness items become owner-review agendas:

- Legal/privacy/safeguarding: owner-led review of risks, assumptions, unresolved questions and limitations. This is not a legal opinion or certification.
- Security: owner-led review of repository evidence, threat/risk records and available synthetic/local test evidence. No independent security assurance is claimed.
- Accessibility: owner-led review of available evidence and checklist. No manual-assessment or WCAG conformance claim unless supported by evidence actually obtained.
- Human operations, hosted isolation, recovery, release and execution remain distinct evidence/authorization gates. This decision does **not** waive, accept, authorize or perform them.
- No gate is promoted merely by this decision or by automated tests.
