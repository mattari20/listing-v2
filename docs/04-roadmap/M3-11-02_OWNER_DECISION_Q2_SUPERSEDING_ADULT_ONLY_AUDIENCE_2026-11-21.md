# M3-11-02 — Superseding Product Owner Decision Q2: Adult-Only Audience and Access

**Decision date:** 2026-11-21  
**Decision:** Option A — adult-only intended audience and access policy (18+)  
**Supersedes:** Product Owner Q2 Option B decision recorded earlier on 2026-11-21  
**Scope:** Product policy direction only; current M3 acceptance remains restricted and synthetic-only

## 1. Superseding decision

The Product Owner reverses the earlier Q2 Option B choice and selects **Option A**:

- Listing V2 is intended for adults aged 18 and over only.
- Minors are not an intended audience and are not permitted to visit, access, use or create accounts on the service.
- No minor account creation, registration, profile, participation or use is permitted.
- The adult-only policy applies to the intended service and any future public or private user-facing access, subject to the enforcement limitations and review requirements below.

This decision supersedes the earlier Q2 Option B record. Option B must not be treated as the current audience policy.

## 2. Enforcement limitation — do not overclaim

A policy statement alone cannot technically prevent a minor from opening a public website URL or making an unauthenticated request. The current repository-only decision does not implement or prove age assurance, identity verification, access denial or account eligibility controls.

Before any user-facing access is proposed, qualified independent legal/privacy and child-safeguarding review must assess a proportionate, lawful and privacy-preserving way to enforce the 18+ restriction, including how age is established, what evidence is retained, how false declarations and circumvention are handled, and how to avoid collecting excessive identity data. The Product Owner must separately approve any proposed mechanism and data processing.

Until those controls are independently assessed, implemented within a separately authorized scope, and verified, **no public/user access or launch is authorized**. Do not claim that the current system blocks minors.

## 3. Current M3 restrictions remain binding

- No real student, child or guardian personal data is collected, imported, stored, processed or used; synthetic/fictional fixtures only.
- No real user account creation, public registration, participant activity or user-facing acceptance is authorized.
- No child/minor accounts, profiles, access, visits, contact or participation is authorized.
- No public application access, pilot, launch, hosted environment, production/V1/Hostinger access, deployment or real-data activity is authorized.
- Public UGC, reviews, ratings, reporting and moderation remain disabled.
- Deeni Madaris remains default-OFF with zero public exposure.
- Existing no-new-spend, no-external-API/AI/provider and no-scope-expansion restrictions remain in force.

## 4. Relationship to the prior Option A data decision

The earlier accepted data decision remains in force: no real student, child or guardian personal data in current M3; synthetic/fictional data only. This superseding audience decision is consistent with and strengthens that boundary. It does not imply that all developer/reviewer account metadata is non-personal.

## 5. Gate effect

This decision closes the Product Owner's current Q2 audience-policy choice as adult-only (18+) and supersedes the earlier Option B decision. It does not constitute legal advice, independent safeguarding assessment, age-assurance design approval, implementation evidence or authorization for public access.

M3-11-02 / Gate 02 remains **BLOCKED** pending qualified independent legal/privacy and child-safeguarding assessment and subsequent Product Owner disposition. Overall M3 remains **OPEN / PARTIALLY COMPLETE — NOT CLOSED**.
