# M3-11-02 — Product Owner Decision Q2: Intended Student Audience

**Decision date:** 2026-11-21  
**Decision:** Option B — general student audience; minors may be among the intended users  
**Scope:** Product-level intended-audience direction only; current M3 acceptance remains restricted and synthetic-only

## 1. Decision

The Product Owner selects **Option B**: Listing V2's intended audience is a general student audience, and students who are minors may be among the eventual intended users.

This supersedes any earlier wording that categorically described children/minors as outside the intended audience. It does not change the separate current-M3 operational boundary.

## 2. Current M3 restrictions remain binding

- No real student, child or guardian personal data is collected, imported, stored, processed or used in current M3; synthetic/fictional fixtures only.
- No child or student accounts, profiles, registration, messaging, direct contact, tracking or child-specific data collection is activated under current M3.
- No child, student, guardian or other participant recruitment, contact, interview, survey, observation or testing is authorized.
- No public application access, pilot, launch, hosted environment, production/V1/Hostinger access, deployment or real-data activity is authorized.
- Public UGC, reviews, ratings, reporting and moderation remain disabled.
- Deeni Madaris remains default-OFF with zero public exposure.
- Existing no-new-spend, no-external-API/AI/provider and no-scope-expansion restrictions remain in force.

## 3. Interpretation

The intended audience and current product capability are different questions. Option B records the eventual audience direction; it does not assert that the current M3 candidate is safe, ready, legally approved or authorized for use by minors.

Before any child/minor-facing access or participation is proposed, a qualified independent legal/privacy and child-safeguarding reviewer must assess applicable age definitions, foreseeable use, child-specific obligations, age-appropriate design, consent/guardian involvement where applicable, contact and grooming risks, reporting/escalation, staff roles/training, and required technical/operational controls. The reviewer must identify evidence and non-waivable blockers. The Product Owner must then make a separate, explicit decision on the proposed scope and authorization.

No assumption is made that parental consent alone resolves all legal or safeguarding requirements.

## 4. Reconciliation

This decision:
- supersedes the previous owner-scope statement that children are not an intended audience;
- preserves the accepted Option A data boundary: no real student/child/guardian personal data in current M3, synthetic-only;
- preserves the exclusion of child accounts, child participation and real-data processing from current M3;
- does not reopen the completed ten-question owner review or earlier Decisions 1–3;
- does not approve any implementation, environment, pilot, public access or release.

## 5. Gate status

This closes the Product Owner's Q2 intended-audience choice only. It is not an independent safeguarding determination, legal opinion, PIA/DPIA, child-safety certification or implementation acceptance.

M3-11-02 / Gate 02 remains **BLOCKED** pending qualified independent legal/privacy and child-safeguarding assessment and subsequent Product Owner disposition. Overall M3 remains **OPEN / PARTIALLY COMPLETE — NOT CLOSED**.

## 6. Status — SUPERSEDED on 2026-11-21

The Product Owner subsequently reversed this Option B decision and selected adult-only Option A (18+). See [Superseding Product Owner Decision Q2 — Adult-Only Audience and Access](M3-11-02_OWNER_DECISION_Q2_SUPERSEDING_ADULT_ONLY_AUDIENCE_2026-11-21.md).

**This Option B decision is historical only and is not the current audience policy.** Do not use it as authorization or product direction. The current policy is adult-only; minors are not permitted to visit, access, use or create accounts on Listing V2. The current M3 no-real-child/student/guardian-data and synthetic-only restrictions remain in force.
