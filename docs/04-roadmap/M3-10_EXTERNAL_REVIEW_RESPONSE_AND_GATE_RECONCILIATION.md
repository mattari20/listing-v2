## 3. Controlled intake requirements

When a human decision or review is independently received through an owner-approved process, record only the minimum necessary metadata in the repository:
1. Intake ID and date received.
2. Decision/review stream and the exact gate(s) it addresses.
3. Reviewer/decision-maker role and qualification/authority confirmation, without unnecessary personal details.
4. Artifact title, version/date and an approved repository reference or controlled evidence reference.
5. Scope, environment, evidence examined, assumptions and limitations.
6. Findings, severity/risk, conditions and required actions.
7. Named human owner for each action, accepted by that person outside this automated record.
8. Reviewer/owner disposition and date.
9. Whether a separate authorization is still required.

Do not commit personal data, participant details, identity documents, contact details, credentials, secrets, private records, raw interview/session recordings or sensitive legal correspondence. Do not upload such materials to ChatGPT or the repository as part of this agenda. If a source artifact cannot safely be retained, record a minimal reference and a qualified human custodian's confirmation through an approved channel; do not invent a link or claim to have inspected it.

A review is not accepted merely because it is received. Its scope and competence must match the gate; conditions and material findings must be addressed by accountable humans; and any required owner decision must be explicit and dated.

## 4. Review response workflow

For each intake item:

1. **Register:** Assign a stable ID (for example, EXT-REV-001) and preserve the received date and artifact version.
2. **Validate scope:** Confirm the author’s stated role/qualification, independence where required, jurisdiction, review scope, environment and limitations. Do not independently certify qualifications.
3. **Map:** Link each finding to M10-G01–G14 and the relevant M10 package. A single review may affect multiple gates.
4. **Classify:** Mark each finding as blocking, conditionally acceptable, advisory or out of scope. Record the reviewer's own severity labels without silently translating them.
5. **Assign:** Record the human action owner only after that person has explicitly accepted the responsibility. ChatGPT is not an owner or approver.
6. **Respond:** Record the human disposition: accepted, remediation required, clarification requested, rejected with rationale, or deferred. Do not resolve professional judgments on behalf of the reviewer or Product Owner.
7. **Verify:** For any later synthetic code remediation, require a separate explicit scope authorization, exact-head CI and documentation evidence. Human/professional approval cannot be inferred from CI.
8. **Reconcile:** Update the gate only when its own closure evidence is present and the accountable Product Owner has accepted any required disposition.
9. **Re-authorize:** Even if all applicable reviews eventually pass, pilot activity and M3-11 require separate explicit written authorization. This agenda cannot grant it.

Silence, missing responses, conditional approval, unresolved high-risk findings, an unassigned action owner or a review outside its stated scope leaves the affected gate open or blocked.
