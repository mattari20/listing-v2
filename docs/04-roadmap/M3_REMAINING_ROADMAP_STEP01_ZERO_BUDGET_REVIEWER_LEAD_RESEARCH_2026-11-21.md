## 4. Mandatory acceptance checks before any reviewer is appointed

For each proposed individual, retain a restricted verification record covering:

1. Identity, organization/affiliation and verifiable professional profile.
2. Relevant qualifications and demonstrated work in the exact discipline.
3. Independence from the project, its implementation and its owner; written conflict-of-interest declaration.
4. Explicit written confirmation of **PKR 0 total cost**, including no travel, administration, tools or indirect charges.
5. Acceptance of the existing workstream-specific scope, evidence list, report format and limitations.
6. Written confidentiality and secure evidence-handling agreement before any non-public material is shared.
7. Agreement that only repository evidence and permitted local synthetic fixtures are in scope; no V1, Hostinger, production, hosted environment, live database, real/source/personal/child data, external API/AI, deployment or dynamic non-local testing.
8. A dated written report that separates evidence examined, work performed/not performed, limitations, findings and conclusions.

A person or organization must not review its own implementation work or claim independence where a material conflict exists. Combined reviewers must separately demonstrate competence for each discipline and produce separate conclusions.

## 5. Recommended no-fee engagement approach

If outreach is later undertaken, use a short, non-confidential expression-of-interest message first. It should state that the request is voluntary and strictly PKR 0; summarize the exact workstream; describe the repository-only/synthetic evidence boundary; ask for relevant qualifications, conflicts and availability; and share no project evidence until confidentiality and scope are accepted.

Do not send the complete private evidence bundle in an initial inquiry. Do not create accounts, pay fees, offer reimbursements, disclose private materials, or accept a reviewer solely because they volunteer.
