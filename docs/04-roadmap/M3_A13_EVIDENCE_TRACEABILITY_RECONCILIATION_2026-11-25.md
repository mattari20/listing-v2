## Evidence classification
Evidence for future acceptance must be classified as:
- **Observed:** directly retrieved/inspected in the relevant current context.
- **Executed:** a test/check actually run, with exact commit, result and scope.
- **Recorded historical:** previously documented evidence; not re-run here.
- **Documentary:** policy, checklist, risk or decision record.
- **Unknown/unavailable:** evidence not obtained or not reported.
- **Deferred:** intentionally reserved for separately authorized target/candidate work.

Do not treat documentary or historical records as current execution evidence. Unknown and pending do not equal passed.
