# M3 Q5 — Product Owner Decision: Combined Independent Reviewer Staffing Model

**Decision date:** 2026-11-21  
**Decision:** ACCEPTED — OPTION B  
**Scope:** Reviewer staffing-model policy and repository preparation only.

## Product Owner decision

The Product Owner selects **Option B — combined reviewers permitted**.

One reviewer may assess more than one of the following disciplines only where that reviewer's competence, relevant experience and independence are evidenced and assessed separately for every discipline assigned:

1. Legal, privacy and child safeguarding.
2. Security.
3. Accessibility.

A combined reviewer is not automatically considered qualified across disciplines merely because they hold one relevant credential, have general professional experience, or are capable in one area. Each discipline must have a distinct scope, evidence basis, findings, limitations and written conclusion. Where competence or independence is not established for a discipline, that discipline must be assigned to a separately qualified independent reviewer or remain unreviewed and blocked.

## Required screening evidence

Before any future appointment can be considered, the candidate-specific record must separately document:

- Verified identity and professional/organizational affiliation, using an appropriate method approved for that engagement.
- Current, relevant qualifications, demonstrated work history and discipline-specific experience.
- The precise assessment scope and methods the candidate is competent to perform.
- Independence and conflict-of-interest disclosure for each assigned discipline, including prior involvement, financial or organizational relationships, and other material conflicts.
- Confidentiality obligations, secure evidence-handling method and permitted recipients.
- Deliverables: separately attributable findings and conclusions for each discipline, evidence reviewed, methods, exclusions, limitations, severity/risk rationale, remediation and retest needs.
- Availability and written willingness to accept the exact scope.
- Cost/expense declaration and confirmation that the existing **PKR 0 / no-fee-only** boundary is respected.

No single credential or self-description is sufficient evidence by itself. Missing, ambiguous, expired or conflicting evidence must be treated as not verified.

## Boundaries and non-effects

- This decision does not identify, qualify, select, contact, appoint or engage any reviewer.
- Existing public leads remain unverified leads, not recommendations or endorsements.
- No outreach, interview, reference check, procurement, fee, honorarium, reimbursement or other spend is authorized.
- No confidential evidence may be shared under this decision. The repository is public; confidential reports and sensitive evidence must not be committed to public repository history.
- No real/source/personal/student/child/guardian/institute data, external API/AI, hosted/V1/Hostinger/production access, live database, migration, deployment, pilot or release is authorized.
- The existing reviewer priority order remains: legal/privacy/child safeguarding first, security second, accessibility third. Priority does not waive any workstream.
- Q3 remains binding: technically enforceable and objectively verified adult-only restriction is a prerequisite to any public/user-facing access, pilot or launch. No age-assurance implementation or age-data processing is authorized by Q5.
- No M3 gate is promoted by this decision or by documentation/CI.
