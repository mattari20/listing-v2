## 4. Reviewer verification and evidence acceptance checklist

No candidate name or qualification is presumed. Before engagement, the Product Owner must verify and retain in an appropriate restricted record:

1. Reviewer identity and organization.
2. Demonstrable competence, relevant experience and jurisdiction/specialism for the assigned workstream.
3. Independence and written conflict-of-interest declaration.
4. Exact scope, reviewed revision, target category, methods, exclusions and limitations.
5. Written acceptance of deliverables, report format, confidentiality and evidence-handling channel.
6. Any fee, cost ceiling, procurement terms and payment authority.
7. Whether additional specialists are needed to cover a distinct discipline.

After the engagement, each workstream must return a dated, signed report identifying the evidence actually examined, tests performed and not performed, assumptions, limitations, findings, severity, conditions, remediation criteria, retest requirements and explicit conclusion. Confidential reports and exploit-sensitive material must remain outside public repository history. A separate Product Owner disposition must then record acceptance, conditions, rejection or deferral without editing or misrepresenting the reviewer’s findings.
