## 7. Minimum evidence required to reopen activation

A future request must first provide, for each source separately:

1. Verified source owner/operator, exact canonical asset, record scope and field allowlist.
2. Dated terms/license and evidence supporting each permitted act, including commercial display/reuse where relevant.
3. Approved access method, rate/volume/cadence, technical restrictions and source-specific expiry.
4. Qualified legal/privacy disposition, field-level data minimization, excluded data, retention/deletion and transfer analysis.
5. Independent security review of retrieval, parsing, SSRF/redirect controls, hostile content, secrets, logging, quotas and kill switch.
6. Exact model/provider/version/region or explicitly approved offline alternative, with license, training/retention, subprocessors, security and exit terms.
7. Synthetic evaluation corpus, independently approved thresholds, extraction/provenance/entity-resolution/freshness/adversarial results and failure handling.
8. Named and accepted human governance, data adjudication, publication, operations and incident roles.
9. Zero-new-spend feasibility evidence or a separate explicit budget authorization.
10. Separate Product Owner authorization naming source, asset, fields, purpose, method, model, environment, cadence, limits, expiry and kill-switch owner.

A changed source, field, method, provider, model version, data class, purpose, region or environment requires reassessment. No approval transfers automatically.
