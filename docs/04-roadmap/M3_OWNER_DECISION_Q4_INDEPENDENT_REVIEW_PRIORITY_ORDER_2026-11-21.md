# M3 — Product Owner Decision Q4: Independent Review Priority Order

**Decision date:** 2026-11-21  
**Decision:** ACCEPTED — OPTION A  
**Authority:** Product Owner  
**Status:** Binding priority decision; preparation only

## 1. Decision

The Product Owner approves the following order for independent-review selection and preparation:

1. **Legal, privacy and child-safeguarding** — first priority.
2. **Security** — second priority.
3. **Accessibility** — third priority.

The sequence reflects dependency and risk priority. It does not remove, narrow or waive any review workstream. Each workstream still requires a reviewer with demonstrable competence for that discipline and a separate, explicit conclusion.

## 2. Boundaries

The existing no-fee-only budget remains **PKR 0**. No honorarium, consulting fee, travel reimbursement, paid tool, procurement, vendor, indirect cost or other expenditure is approved.

This decision authorizes prioritization and repository-side preparation only. It does not authorize:
- contacting, inviting, selecting, appointing, contracting, briefing or paying any reviewer;
- sharing confidential repository material, evidence, credentials or reports;
- collecting reviewer personal data beyond any separately approved process;
- external assessment, legal advice, penetration testing, hosted/dynamic testing or manual accessibility testing;
- V1, Hostinger, production, hosted environment, live database, backup/restore or deployment access;
- real/source/student/child/guardian/personal data, participant activity, external API/AI, pilot, public access or release.

Any reviewer engagement requires a separate exact-scope Product Owner authorization covering the reviewer identity, competence/independence verification, scope, confidentiality and evidence channel, permitted methods, deliverables, cost ceiling and stop conditions.

## 3. Gate effect

Q4 closes the owner priority decision only. It does not appoint reviewers or produce independent evidence.

- M3-11-02 / Gate 02 legal/privacy/safeguarding: **BLOCKED / NOT ACCEPTED**.
- M3-11-03 independent security/accessibility assurance: **BLOCKED / NOT ACCEPTED**.
- Overall M3: **OPEN / PARTIALLY COMPLETE — NOT CLOSED**.

## 4. Relationship to earlier decisions

Q4 follows Q3 Option A, which makes a technically enforceable and verified adult-only restriction mandatory before public/user-facing access, pilot or launch. Q4 does not authorize designing or implementing an age-assurance mechanism; qualified review and separate exact-scope approval remain prerequisites.

The earlier PKR 0 no-fee-only boundary remains unchanged. Q4 does not reopen Q1, Q2 or Q3.
