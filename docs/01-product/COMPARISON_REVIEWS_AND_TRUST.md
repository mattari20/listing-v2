# Institution Comparison, Ratings, Reviews and Trust

## Comparison
Allow students to compare a small, usable shortlist (target 3–5; confirm through product validation) across:
- Distance and location.
- Institution type/sector and education level.
- Exact programme and campus availability.
- Eligibility, duration and admission status.
- Published fees with period and effective date.
- Recognition/affiliation verification.
- Facilities, transport, hostel and scholarships.
- Review dimensions and review count.

Comparison must show missing or stale values instead of treating them as zero. Ranking and scoring methodology must be explainable; no paid result may be presented as organic.

## Ratings and reviews — product requirement
Ratings and written reviews are part of the V2 product model and are intended for use after the platform launches. They are not a pre-launch research survey.

- Visitors may browse public institute information without submitting a review.
- A visitor must create an account or log in before submitting a rating or written review.
- A submitted review must be associated with the authenticated account; public display should not expose private account details by default.
- The final eligibility rule (for example, whether any account may review or whether evidence of experience is required) must be decided before implementation. Do not label a person a verified student without a proportionate, privacy-preserving verification process and evidence.
- Candidate rating dimensions: teaching quality, faculty, facilities, administration, fee value, learning environment and student support. Final dimensions, scale and any weighting require a documented V2 decision; do not imply a statistically representative score.
- Show rating count and context alongside averages. Explain calculation and treatment of missing, disputed or removed reviews.
- Prevent duplicate, spam, coordinated, incentivized-without-disclosure and conflicted submissions through proportionate controls.
- Provide reporting, moderation, appeal and correction processes. Institutions may have a right of reply but must not have unilateral deletion power.
- Publish clear community guidelines and moderation reasons. Moderate threats, harassment, personal data, unlawful content and credible defamation risks.
- Do not collect unnecessary identity or enrollment documents. Any verification must minimize data, restrict access and define retention/deletion.

## Research and timing boundary
No survey, interview, participant recruitment or outreach will be conducted during construction. Ratings and reviews are a post-launch product feature through authenticated user accounts. Product design and safety requirements may be specified now; real user feedback will be collected through the platform after launch. Any later separate research activity would require a new explicit owner decision.

## Minors and privacy
The platform may be used by minors. Before implementation or release of user-generated content, determine applicable age requirements, consent and safeguarding duties, data minimization, retention limits, reporting/escalation procedures and jurisdiction-specific legal requirements. Do not assume that account login alone satisfies these requirements.
