# Product Vision and Student Journey

## Vision
A student-first education discovery, admissions information and comparison platform that helps learners and families make informed choices about where and what to study.

## Primary student journey — provisional desk-research model
1. Choose a pathway-led start (current stage/qualification and intended next step) or go directly to search.
2. Discover institutions, campuses and programmes by name, subject, level, type, sector and locality.
3. Use manual locality search or opt into nearby discovery; location permission is never mandatory.
4. Inspect the relevant institution, campus and programme information without assuming every campus offers every programme.
5. Check eligibility, duration, subjects, admission status, deadlines, total cost, scholarships and recognition/affiliation where verified.
6. Compare a shortlist using like-for-like criteria and visible missing/stale data.
7. Verify important claims through cited official sources or clearly identified provider-submitted information.
8. Follow an official contact/application route; V2 does not imply it submits an application.
9. Save/share options and opt into useful updates where supported.
10. After launch, authenticated users may submit reviews/ratings and corrections under moderation and privacy controls.

This journey is a provisional requirements model based on desk research and owner direction. It has not been validated with participants. Primary research is deferred during construction.

## Audiences
- Students transitioning between school, college, university or vocational pathways.
- Parents/guardians supporting student decisions.
- Institutions maintaining verified profiles and responding to feedback.
- Researchers and education stakeholders using appropriately licensed aggregate information.

## Product principles
- Mobile-friendly, accessible, low-bandwidth and text-first; do not assume every learner has a personal device or reliable connectivity.
- Evaluate Urdu and English support during requirements/design; do not claim validated language preference.
- Clear distinction between official facts, institution-submitted claims, student opinions, AI-discovered candidates and estimates.
- Explain freshness, source and verification status for material facts.
- No misleading paid ranking or hidden sponsorship.
- Protect minors and personal information; minimize collection of student data.
- Manual locality search remains available if precise location is declined.
- Student segments and the journey remain provisional until separately authorized participant validation.

## Scope categories to assess
Early childhood/pre-primary; primary; middle; secondary/matric; higher secondary/intermediate; colleges; universities and campuses; technical/vocational/skills; teacher training; special education; non-formal/adult learning; madaris/religious education and other recognized providers. Final taxonomy and inclusion rules are M1 deliverables.
