# Product Vision and Student Journey

## Vision
A student-first education discovery, admissions information and comparison platform that helps learners and families make informed choices about where and what to study.

## Primary student journey
1. State current education level/class, completed qualification and intended next step.
2. Explore suitable education pathways and programmes.
3. Search nearby government and private institutions by location, distance, level, sector and programme.
4. Inspect institution and campus profiles.
5. Review programme details, eligibility, duration, subjects, fees, admission status, deadlines, affiliation/recognition and scholarships where verified.
6. Compare a shortlist using transparent criteria.
7. Contact the institution or follow its official application instructions.
8. Save institutions/programmes and optionally receive relevant admission updates.
9. After a genuine experience, submit a review/rating and report inaccurate information.

## Audiences
- Students transitioning between school, college, university or vocational pathways.
- Parents/guardians supporting student decisions.
- Institutions maintaining verified profiles and responding to feedback.
- Researchers and education stakeholders using appropriately licensed aggregate information.

## Product principles
- Mobile-first, accessible, low-bandwidth friendly.
- Urdu and English support should be evaluated during requirements design; English is the initial documentation language.
- Clear distinction between official facts, institution-submitted claims, student opinions, AI-discovered candidates and estimates.
- Explain freshness, source and verification status.
- No misleading paid ranking or hidden sponsorship.
- Protect minors and personal information; minimize collection of student data.

## Scope categories to assess
Early childhood/pre-primary; primary; middle; secondary/matric; higher secondary/intermediate; colleges; universities and campuses; technical/vocational/skills; teacher training; special education; non-formal/adult learning; madaris/religious education and other recognized providers. Final taxonomy and inclusion rules are M1 deliverables.
