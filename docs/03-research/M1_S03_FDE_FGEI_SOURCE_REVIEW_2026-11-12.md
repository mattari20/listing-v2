# M1-S03 — Federal Directorate of Education and FGEI Source Review

**Review date:** 2026-11-12  
**Scope:** Federal Directorate of Education (FDE) ICT school/college routes and Federal Government Educational Institutions (Cantonments/Garrisons) directorate (FGEI C/G).  
**Status:** Supplemental official-source research; M1-S03 remains in progress.

## 1. Federal Directorate of Education (FDE) — ICT

**Official routes:** [FDE Education Transformation Portal](https://www.fde.edu.pk/), [FDE 2026–27 admissions portal](https://eadmissions.fde.gov.pk/portal), [FDE GIS-based EMIS](https://gsc1.sgs-suparco.gov.pk/gemis/default.aspx).

The current FDE Education Transformation Portal describes a unified support platform for FDE schools and colleges and displays **432 FDE institutions**. It also displays student/personnel aggregates and education intervention information. The FDE GIS-based EMIS route is an official FDE-branded system hosted on a SUparco domain and describes institute details, annual census, staff management, assets and transport modules. The 2026–27 FDE admission portal is reported as publicly accessible and provides institution selection, real-time seat availability and merit-list functions.

**V2 interpretation and limitations**
- The 432 figure is a portal headline, not a record-level public institution directory or proof of a complete, current school/college register. The portal does not show a clear as-of date for this figure.
- The Education Transformation Portal is a programme/support gateway, not an institution register.
- The admission portal is an admissions/seat-availability source for the 2026–27 session, not a general institution registry. Do not collect applicant identities, merit-list personal data or child/student records.
- The GIS-based EMIS includes staff-profile, assets and transport modules. Do not access or collect staff personal details, driver identities, vehicle registration data or other sensitive operational information. Public institution metadata availability and reuse terms were not established.
- FDE jurisdiction is ICT; do not treat the 432 FDE network as a national/federal network outside ICT.
- FDE must remain separate from FGEI C/G, which has its own directorate and nationwide cantonment/garrison regional footprint.

**Disposition:** Official FDE portal and admissions/EMIS routes identified; provider-level public register and source rights remain unverified. **PARTIAL / RESEARCH ONLY**.

## 2. Federal Government Educational Institutions (Cantonments/Garrisons) — FGEI C/G

**Official routes:** [FGEI portal](https://fgei.gov.pk/), [FGEI colleges](https://www.fgei.gov.pk/colleges.php).

FGEI C/G is a distinct federal education directorate under the Inspector General Training & Evaluation (IGT&E) Branch, not part of FDE. Its portal describes schools and colleges across regional offices including Peshawar, Wah, Rawalpindi, Chaklala, Kharian, Lahore, Multan, Bahawalpur, Gujranwala, Karachi, Fazaia and Quetta. The indexed official portal reports **311 schools and 49 colleges**. Its colleges page reports 35 degree colleges, 12 intermediate colleges and two postgraduate colleges (49 in total).

**V2 interpretation and limitations**
- FGEI C/G must be modeled as a distinct provider/administrative network, not merged into FDE or ICT-only institutions.
- The 311/49 headline and 35/12/2 college breakdown are official indexed page snapshots; no common as-of date was visible in the indexed content.
- Direct page opening was access-limited during this review (HTTP 403), so the underlying record-level directory, current operational status, campus details and terms could not be independently inspected.
- Regional footprint spans multiple provinces/territories and cantonment/garrison contexts; geography must be attached to each institution, not inferred from the directorate headquarters.
- No source-specific reuse license was verified.

**Disposition:** Official source lead and aggregate/category breakdown observed; record-level access and rights remain unverified. **PARTIAL / ACCESS-LIMITED / RESEARCH ONLY**.

## 3. Federal source separation

| Source | Administrative scope | What it provides | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|---|
| FDE | ICT public education network | 432-institution headline, programme/support portal, admissions and GIS/EMIS routes | National federal coverage, open record-level register, unrestricted data reuse |
| FGEI C/G | Federal cantonment/garrison school and college network across regions | 311-school/49-college headline and college-category breakdown | FDE affiliation, ICT-only jurisdiction, record-level completeness or current operational status |
| FBISE | Examination-board affiliation across multiple jurisdictions and overseas | SSC/HSSC-affiliated institution codes/names/levels | Provider registration, HEC recognition, FDE/FGEI administrative control or programme accreditation |

These source families must not be combined into one "federal school count" without explicit scope, date and entity reconciliation.
