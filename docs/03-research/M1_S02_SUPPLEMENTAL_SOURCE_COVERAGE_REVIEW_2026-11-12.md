# M1-S02 — Supplemental Official-Source Coverage Review

**Review date:** 2026-11-12  
**Scope:** Documentation-only desk research. No data collection, scraping, downloads, external contacts, permission requests, source registration, connector activation, or implementation.  
**Decision status:** Evidence supplement only; it does not complete S02 or approve any source for reuse.

## 1. New and clarified official source evidence

### AJK school providers
The AJK Elementary & Secondary Education Department currently advertises a **List of Registered Private Educational Institutes in AJK**. Its News & Notifications page dates that item to **2025-11-05** and separately lists guidance for the first private-institution educational census 2024–25. The department also announced a changed PEIRA/EMIS census portal address. This materially strengthens the AJK private-school source lead, but it does not prove that the list is current at record level, covers every district, includes public schools, or grants reuse rights.

Sources:
- https://ese.gok.pk/
- https://www.ese.gok.pk/news-and-notifications

### Balochistan school EMIS
The public School Profile Search exposes BEMIS code, school name, legal status, district, tehsil, level, gender and status filters. The official EMIS landing page separately exposes School Sections, Private Schools, NFE, GIS and Downloads. This confirms distinct navigation/source families and useful record dimensions; it does not establish that every route is populated, complete, downloadable, current, or licensed for reuse.

Sources:
- https://www.emis.gob.pk/views/Reports/Reports/SchoolSearchPublic.aspx
- https://emis.gob.pk/
- https://www.emis.gob.pk/website/SchoolSections.aspx

### National non-formal education information
NFEMIS publishes a national dashboard and a school-inventory table. The dashboard currently reports 20,862 of 36,272 schools and 144 of 166 organizations, while the inventory table dated 2026-09-21 reports category counts of 18,630 NFE primary, 1,406 NFE middle, 469 adult and 359 other (20,864 in total). The two displayed school totals differ by two. These are dashboard-reported figures, not an independently verified census; the discrepancy must be reconciled before using totals. NFEMIS includes all provinces and territories in its displayed distribution, strengthening the national NFE source-family lead without proving full provider coverage or permission to reuse.

Sources:
- https://www.nfemis.net/Dashboard.aspx
- https://www.nfemis.net/

### Gilgit-Baltistan EMIS freshness warning
The GB EMIS dashboard endpoint currently renders zero values for school, staff and student summary metrics. A separate schools/details route has been identified, but the zero-valued dashboard means the summary cannot be used as evidence of current coverage or counts. The source requires a functioning record-level check, publication date, scope statement and terms review before it can support a completeness claim.

Sources:
- https://emis2.gilgitbaltistan.gov.pk/
- https://emis.gilgitbaltistan.gov.pk/dashboard/schools/details

### HEC qualification and allied-health NOC distinctions
HEC's Pakistan Qualification Register describes quality-assured higher qualifications and the providers/HEIs recognized or accredited to deliver and issue them. HEC separately publishes DAIs granted NOCs in Allied Health Sciences with named institutions, NOC dates, addresses and allowed programmes. These are distinct qualification and programme/NOC evidence layers; neither should be collapsed into a generic institution-recognition flag. The Allied Health Professionals Council Act, 2022 defines allied-health disciplines, but the statute is not a current recognized-institute directory.

Sources:
- https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/universities/pqr/Pages/default.aspx
- https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/universities/Pages/DAIs/NOC-AHS.aspx
- https://www.pakistancode.gov.pk/pdffiles/administrator85ca72c783ea32f5ab4b113a704d9662.pdf

### Special-education evidence remains historical or incomplete
A KP government-hosted parliamentary attachment lists special-education institutions by district for the 2020–21 student-support year; it is useful as a historical lead, not a current register. A national parliamentary answer reports an older province-wise count of 81 institutions, also historical. Neither source establishes current operating status, current ownership, complete district coverage or reuse permission. No current complete multi-jurisdiction special-education directory was verified in this review.

Sources:
- https://www.pakp.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/14398.pdf
- https://senate.gov.pk/uploads/documents/debates/1433744497_947.pdf

## 2. Updated coverage assessment

- AJK private-school coverage is now supported by a dated official register announcement, but public-school coverage and record-level freshness remain unverified.
- Balochistan EMIS exposes richer school/legal-status fields and separate private-school/NFE routes; actual completeness and access rights remain unverified.
- NFEMIS is a stronger national NFE discovery lead, but its own displayed school totals differ by two between dashboard and dated inventory views.
- GB EMIS dashboard summary metrics are currently zero-valued and cannot support coverage or count claims.
- HEC PQR and allied-health NOC pages add qualification-level and programme-specific evidence layers.
- Current special-education registers outside Punjab and Sindh remain unverified; historical documents are not accepted as current coverage evidence.

## 3. Rights, privacy and use boundary

The review observed public-facing government and regulator pages only. It did not identify a source-specific license or written permission for automated collection, bulk download, storage, republication or commercial reuse. Public visibility and dashboard access are not reuse authorization. Personal staff names, direct phone numbers, student-level data, student profiles, staff profiles and any sensitive disability/health information are excluded from the V2 institution-discovery research scope. All source families remain **RESEARCH ONLY**.

## 4. Acceptance implications

This supplement improves source-family discovery and records explicit freshness/data-quality warnings. It does not establish a complete national provider census, complete public/private coverage, record-by-record status verification, legal clearance, or S02 acceptance. S02-C04, C06, C07 and C08 remain in progress; S02-C09 remains an owner-only decision gate.
