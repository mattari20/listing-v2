# M1-S02 — Consolidated National Institution Coverage and Gap Matrix

**Review date:** 2026-11-12  
**Purpose:** Reconcile the independent source-family research into one jurisdiction-by-sector view for M1-S02.  
**Status:** Research baseline with explicit gaps; not a complete national census, approved taxonomy, legal opinion or authorization to collect/reuse data.  
**Independence:** Based on V2 owner requirements and newly consulted public official sources; no legacy repository data, architecture or implementation patterns used.

## 1. How to read this matrix

The status labels describe evidence of a source route, not proof that every provider in a jurisdiction is represented.

- **Strong source route:** an official public register/search/list with identifiable institution-level records was observed. Completeness, freshness and reuse rights may still be unresolved.
- **Partial source route:** an official source or list exists but only covers a subset, has material limitations, is role-based, is aggregate-only, is stale or requires scope reconciliation.
- **Gap / not verified:** no current complete public institution register was established in this research pass. This is not a claim that providers do not exist.
- **Excluded evidence:** staging/sample, historical-only, proposed projects or non-institution records that must not be used as current provider coverage evidence.

## 4. Cross-cutting data-quality findings that must not be normalized away

1. **NFEMIS:** dashboard reports 20,862 schools while 2026-09-21 inventory categories sum to 20,864. Preserve both as source-reported observations until reconciled.
2. **GB EMIS:** summary dashboard displays zero-valued school/staff/student metrics, while a details route returns school and office records. Do not use zero summaries as counts; filter administrative offices from school records.
3. **AJK HED:** official pages state 178 and 180 colleges. Do not choose either without an official explanation; category lists may overlap.
4. **B-TEVTA:** public directory shows 16; NAVTTC overview cites 80+. Different/unclear scopes; not comparable totals.
5. **NAVTTC:** 650+ accredited, 900+ quality-assured and 3,500+ ecosystem institutes are distinct/undefined categories; do not combine or substitute them.
6. **DGRE:** regional Madaris lists surfaced with a common 26 March 2024 date and contain cross-province/address and incomplete-row anomalies; not a verified 2026 national census.
7. **Punjab special education:** service-category counts may overlap; do not sum into unique facility total.
8. **GB special education:** centre location descriptions and event references require reconciliation.
9. **Sindh private schools:** older official census TOR notes registered and unregistered providers and the need for data updating; current complete register not verified.
10. **KP private schools:** ACTIVE, BLOCKED, DORMANT and CLOSED are separate statuses; district pages do not prove province-wide completeness.
11. **HEC affiliation:** parent university recognition does not prove each affiliated college/study centre/programme has current continuation.
12. **PNMC/PCP/PM&DC and other councils:** institute listing, programme recognition, intake, admission permission, seat allocation, court-order exceptions and individual professional registration are different facts.

## 5. Provisional taxonomy and relationship requirements

The provider family model must include:
- Schools (public/private, primary/middle/secondary/higher-secondary, international curriculum where evidenced)
- Colleges (intermediate, degree, postgraduate, professional, affiliated and constituent)
- Universities and DAIs
- Technical/vocational/skills providers, apprenticeship providers and assessment centres
- Non-formal/adult literacy/community education providers
- Special-education institutes and service centres
- Deeni Madaris as a distinct provider family
- Distance/open-learning providers and separately identified study/examination centres
- Professional/specialist providers by regulated discipline

Keep these as distinct entities/relationships:
1. Provider
2. Campus/branch/study centre
3. Programme/course and intake/session
4. Registration/regulator relationship
5. Affiliation relationship
6. Examination/assessment board relationship
7. Qualification-awarding body
8. Specific qualification equivalence
9. Programme accreditation/approval
10. Evidence source, effective period, checked date and status history

Do not expose unnecessary personal or sensitive attributes (student/teacher profiles, CNICs, personal mobile numbers, individual health/disability details or DGRE maslak/sect) in the general institution-discovery model.

## 6. Rights and privacy conclusion

No blanket national open-data permission for education directories was identified. MoITT's official registers list the National Data Governance Policy 2026 and Personal Data Protection Bill May 2023 as Draft. RTI access is not a blanket republication/commercial-reuse license. Copyright in compilations and underlying data/material requires separate analysis. PBS's open license is scoped to specified PBS products/tiers and does not extend to HEC, PEIRA, EMIS, professional councils or TEVTA directories.

**Decision for all candidate education sources: RESEARCH ONLY.** No source-specific license or written permission was established in this review. No source owner was contacted, no permission request was sent, and no data was collected.
