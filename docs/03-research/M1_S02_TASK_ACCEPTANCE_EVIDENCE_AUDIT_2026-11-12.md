## 1. Completion rule

A research task may be marked complete when its specified source mapping, taxonomy, status semantics, rights assessment or synthesis has been documented with explicit limitations. A source gap or missing license can be a completed research finding if the task requires documenting that gap and the access/rights boundary. "Completed" does not mean every institution record has been harvested, every regulator has granted permission, or every provider is independently verified.

S02-C01 and S02-C09 require explicit owner review/approval and must not be self-approved by the assistant.
