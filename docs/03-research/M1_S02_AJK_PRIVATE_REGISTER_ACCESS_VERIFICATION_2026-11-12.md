# M1-S02 — AJK Private-School Register Access Verification

**Review date:** 2026-11-12  
**Scope:** Verify the access path for AJK's officially announced registered-private-institution list. No sign-in, credential use, data download, scraping, external contact or permission request was attempted.  
**Status:** Research-only; source access remains unresolved.

## 1. Official announcement verified

AJK Elementary & Secondary Education Department's News & Notifications page lists **Registered Private Educational Institutes in AJ&K**, posted **5 November 2025** by Secretariat (PEIRA), and links it to a Google Drive PDF. It separately lists 2024–25 private and public education-census guidance. The department homepage announces that the private-institute EMIS census portal address changed to https://peira.ese.gok.pk.

Sources:
- https://www.ese.gok.pk/news-and-notifications
- https://ese.gok.pk/

## 2. Anonymous access check — not publicly verified

The linked Google Drive PDF opens to a sign-in page in the available public browser review rather than a readable/downloadable register. The announced PEIRA portal redirects to a login page with username/email and password fields. No public, unauthenticated list/search route was verified.

Observed access state:
- **Announcement page:** public and readable.
- **Register PDF link:** linked from official page, but browser view prompts Google sign-in; file contents and exact row count could not be verified.
- **PEIRA portal:** login-only in the reviewed route; no public directory route verified.
- **2024–25 census guidance:** its linked Google Drive file also prompts sign-in in the reviewed browser view.

This changes the evidence classification: AJK has an official, dated announcement of a private-school register, but not yet a verified public-access register. Do not call it a strong public register or claim record-level coverage until the file is accessible through an authorized public route or the source owner confirms an access method.

## 3. Data rights and privacy implications

- The existence of an official list announcement does not prove anonymous access, a public download, an API or a reuse license.
- The PEIRA portal's login requirement must not be bypassed; no credentials were requested or used.
- The Google Drive sign-in prompt is an access limitation, not proof that the document is confidential or that access is denied to every authorized user.
- No source-specific license or written permission for automated collection, storage, republication or commercial use was established.
- Do not seek or collect CNIC/mobile identifiers or student/teacher records from the census process.

## 4. Effect on M1-S02

- **S02-C04:** AJK private-school coverage must be downgraded from "strong public register" to **partial official source lead — access unverified**. The dated announcement is verified, but record-level contents, row count, district coverage and current status remain unverified.
- **S02-C07:** access method is login/Google Drive sign-in in the reviewed route; rights remain unresolved.
- **S02-C08:** consolidated matrix and AJK source matrix must reflect that the private register is announced but not publicly inspected.
- **S02-C09:** remains owner-only.

No login, data download, scraping, source-owner contact or permission request was made. All AJK private-school records remain RESEARCH ONLY until access and terms are confirmed.
