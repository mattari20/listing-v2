# M1-S02 — Data Rights, Public-Information Access and Privacy Framework Review

**Review date:** 2026-11-12  
**Scope:** Official policy/statute desk research for source-rights assessment. This is not legal advice or a source-specific legal clearance. No source-owner contact, permission request, collection or implementation was undertaken.  
**Status:** Research-only. No source is approved for automated collection, republication or commercial reuse.

## 1. National data governance policy status

The Ministry of IT & Telecommunication's official Policies register lists the **National Data Governance Policy 2026** as **Draft**, with an approval-date field of 26 June 2026. MoITT's public consultation announcement also calls it a draft and invited feedback. The Pakistan Digital Authority later stated that the feedback period had closed, but its own public page still links to the draft policy. Therefore, do not describe the policy as an approved or in-force open-data authorization at this review date.

The draft policy's stated direction toward data governance, secure sharing and data classification is useful context for future requirements, but it does not replace existing statutes, agency-specific authority or written permission. Its closure of public consultation is not equivalent to formal approval, notification or commencement.

## 2. Personal-data legislation and privacy controls

MoITT's official Legislation register continues to list the **Personal Data Protection Bill May 2023** as **Draft**. The same register lists the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act, 2016 as an approved Act (with its later amendment history). The draft Bill must not be cited as enacted law. The existence of an in-force cybercrime statute must not be mistaken for a comprehensive permission to process personal information or a license to republish institution-directory records.

For V2 research, apply conservative privacy-by-design safeguards regardless of legislative status:
- collect only institution-level fields demonstrably needed for discovery;
- exclude student names, identifiers, profiles, enrolment-level records and individual academic/health records;
- exclude disability/diagnosis/therapy information and individual practitioner or staff profiles;
- exclude personal mobile numbers, personal email addresses and names of principals/heads where institution-level official contact channels are sufficient;
- exclude religious sect/maslak classification from general institution discovery;
- avoid collecting CNICs, personal identity numbers, credentials, passwords or sensitive identifiers;
- define purpose, retention, correction, deletion, access control and incident handling before any later data collection is proposed.

## 3. Right to information is access, not automatic reuse permission

The Pakistan Information Commission describes the Right of Access to Information Act, 2017 as a framework for citizens to access records held by public authorities, subject to lawful restrictions. The Commission also lists the 2019 Rules and 2023 fee regulations. This supports a route for requesting records through lawful public-information procedures where appropriate, but an RTI right to obtain a record does not, by itself, grant a blanket license to reproduce, publish, commercialize or bulk-process the record. Each source's statutory mandate, terms, copyright, confidentiality and privacy restrictions still require assessment.

## 4. Copyright and database compilation distinction

The official Copyright Ordinance, 1962 text states that copyright in a compilation of data or other material does not extend to the data/material itself, without prejudice to any copyright that may subsist in the data/material. This is not a blanket declaration that all public directory data is free to copy. It means the compilation and underlying material require separate analysis; photographs, descriptions, logos, page design, original text, curated classifications and other protected works may retain their own rights. Individual source terms and any statutory restrictions remain relevant.

## 5. A narrow open-license example — Pakistan Bureau of Statistics

The Pakistan Bureau of Statistics' Official Statistics & Data Dissemination Policy describes an open-by-default/confidentiality-by-design approach for PBS-produced official statistics. It says specified Tier 1 and Tier 2 products/Public Use Files carry a standard open government license permitting copying, adaptation, redistribution and commercial/non-commercial use with attribution and other conditions. It also identifies restricted tiers for licensed or non-disseminable microdata.

This is a useful example of explicit, scoped reuse authorization, but it applies to PBS assets covered by that policy. It does not grant rights to HEC, PEIRA, provincial EMIS, professional-council, school, TVET or other third-party directories. PBS products must be classified by tier and their own citation/license conditions before use.

Source:
- https://www.pbs.gov.pk/data-dissemination/

## 6. Source-rights decision framework for M1-S02

For each source, record and verify separately:
1. **Publisher/authority:** which body owns or administers the source and under what mandate.
2. **Source scope:** public/private, jurisdiction, institution types, record-level or aggregate.
3. **Access mode:** public page, searchable register, download, API, authenticated portal, RTI request or direct permission.
4. **Terms/license:** exact license/version and whether commercial reuse, adaptation, storage, republication and redistribution are permitted.
5. **Copyright:** source page, compilation, photographs/logos, descriptions and underlying records.
6. **Privacy/confidentiality:** personal contacts, staff/student/health/disability/religious information and statutory restrictions.
7. **Freshness/quality:** publication date, last-updated date, correction process, source disclaimer, completeness and record anomalies.
8. **Attribution and endorsement:** required citation, change disclosure and no implied official endorsement.
9. **Retention and correction:** allowed caching/retention period, correction/deletion route and source-of-truth link.
10. **Decision:** research only, manual verification permitted, licensed collection permitted, written permission required, or excluded.

A page being publicly viewable, searchable, paginated, indexed or downloadable is not sufficient evidence of a reuse license. A source's explicit open license may be relied upon only within its exact scope and conditions. Where terms are absent or ambiguous, keep the source RESEARCH ONLY and obtain source-owner/legal review before any ingestion or republication.
