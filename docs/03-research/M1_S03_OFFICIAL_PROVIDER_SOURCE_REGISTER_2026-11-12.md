# M1-S03 — Official Government and Regulator Provider Source Register

**Prepared:** 2026-11-12  
**Repository:** `mattari20/listing-v2`  
**Step status:** In progress — initial official-source verification pass  
**Scope:** Official government, statutory authority, regulator, education management information system and qualification/awarding-body source routes in Pakistan.  
**Independence:** Based on V2 owner requirements and independently consulted public official sources. No legacy repository material, code, data, architecture or implementation patterns were used.

## 1. Purpose and evidence rules

This register turns the M1-S02 source-family discovery into source-specific evidence for M1-S03. It records the responsible authority, what the source actually does, the public route observed, visible coverage/granularity, access constraints, freshness evidence, rights position and follow-up gaps.

A public webpage is not proof of an open API, unrestricted scraping, permission to republish, complete coverage or current approval of every listed provider. No source is cleared for ingestion by this register.

**Evidence labels**
- **Official source route observed:** an official government/statutory body page or register was found and its stated function/visible fields reviewed.
- **Partial / aggregate:** source offers aggregate, filtered, historical, sample, partial or limited record-level evidence.
- **Access constrained:** requires institution credentials, authenticated access, login, a formal request or another restricted route.
- **Rights unverified:** no source-specific reuse license/permission verified for institution-level records.
- **RESEARCH ONLY:** may be used for research and manual verification only; no ingestion or republication authorized.

## 3. Deeni Madaris — provider inclusion and visibility separation

The owner has directed that Deeni Madaris remain in V2's underlying provider scope but **not appear in the public listing by default at launch**. A configurable `Show Deeni Madaris` setting is required, defaulting to OFF. The detailed product decision is recorded in `../00-foundation/DECISION_REGISTER.md` and `../01-product/INSTITUTION_AND_PROGRAMME_REQUIREMENTS.md`.

Research distinctions:
- DGRE/MoFEPT registration is the federal Madaris registration route described by DGRE.
- IBCC recognition of a QAB is not automatic recognition/equivalence of every qualification offered by that body.
- HEC states it does not register or affiliate Madaris; it evaluates specified Deeni Asnad from approved institutions under stated conditions.
- The public institution model must not infer religious sect/maslak from a provider name, Wafaq, board, or other affiliation. Do not store sect/maslak as a general discovery attribute.
- Hidden-from-listing is a visibility state, not deletion, rejection, non-recognition or a statement about legal status.

## 4. Cross-source operating rules

1. Store source authority, exact source URL, observed date, source as-of/effective date (if published), source identifier, jurisdiction, data scope, access method, rights decision, privacy classification, last verification date and limitations.
2. Distinguish source types: regulator register, ministry directory, EMIS, aggregate statistics, programme/accreditation register, QAB list, application portal and institutional self-submission.
3. Distinguish provider registration, institutional recognition, campus recognition, programme approval, accreditation, affiliation, examination/assessment authority, QAB status and qualification equivalence.
4. Do not convert dashboard totals, staging/demo records, dated PDFs or partial search results into a verified national provider count.
5. Do not access or retain student, child, teacher, staff, CNIC, health, disability, therapy, payroll, security or other sensitive personal data. For Madaris, do not collect sect/maslak.
6. No automated scraping, bulk download, API activation, login, credential request, source-owner contact or permission request is included in M1-S03 without separate authorization.
7. All sources remain **RESEARCH ONLY** unless a source-specific license/permission is documented. The sole existing scoped exception remains the SED Balochistan dashboard headline metrics and district breakdowns with attribution and an as-of date; it does not extend to school-level records or other sources.
