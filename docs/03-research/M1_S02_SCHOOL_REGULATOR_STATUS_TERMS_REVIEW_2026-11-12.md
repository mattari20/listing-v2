# M1-S02 — School-Regulator Status Semantics and Source-Terms Review

**Review date:** 2026-11-12  
**Scope:** Official public-source desk research only. No scraping, collection, external contact, permission requests or implementation.  
**Status:** Research-only; this does not approve any source for reuse or close M1-S02.

## 1. KPPSRA — registration, renewal, blocked status and board affiliation are separate

KPPSRA's public portal describes its mandate as regulating private schools and explicitly identifies registration and renewal as separate functions. Current notices include 2026–27 school requirements, action against unregistered schools and a notification concerning de-affiliation by BISEs of private schools not registered with KP-PSRA. This demonstrates that a private school's regulator registration and its BISE affiliation/examination relationship are distinct and may affect one another. The public portal also surfaces a district register with ACTIVE, BLOCKED, DORMANT and CLOSED states.

For V2, preserve:
- regulator and registration number;
- status exactly as issued (including blocked/dormant/closed);
- status effective date/last checked date;
- separate BISE/board affiliation and its status;
- school level and campus/branch identity.

Do not treat registration as board affiliation, or board affiliation as a general quality accreditation. The portal's current notices are evidence of regulatory activity, not a complete school census.

Sources:
- https://www.psra.gkp.pk/public/
- https://www.psra.gkp.pk/registers
- https://www.psra.gkp.pk/district/get-schools?district_id=1

## 2. Sindh DIRPIS — current regulatory notices versus historical census documentation

Sindh School Education & Literacy Department's private-institutions notifications page includes October 2026 directions to the Directorate General of Inspection and Registration of Private Institutions regarding dangerous/structurally unsafe private-school buildings and finalization of lists/relocation of students. This is current evidence that private-school regulation and safety oversight remain active. It is not itself a complete list of registered private schools.

The separate 2023 registration criteria and older SPEI-EMIS census TOR remain historical policy/process evidence. The TOR explicitly notes that both registered and unregistered private schools exist and that data required updating. Therefore, current regulatory activity should not be mistaken for a current complete registry; the private-school directory/export, current registration status and update date remain to be verified.

- https://www.sindheducation.gov.pk/Contents/Notifications/86125%20Criteria%20for%20Registration%20of%20Private%20Schools.PDF
- https://www.sindheducation.gov.pk/Contents/Notifications/Bidding%20Documents%20for%20Procurement%20of%20Non-Consultant%20Services.pdf

## 3. HEC Digital Library terms — do not misapply publisher-resource licenses to institution directory

HEC Digital Library terms require participating institutions to comply with individual publisher licenses for subscribed electronic resources, prohibit unauthorized copying/trading of those licensed resources and require copyright respect. These terms govern Digital Library subscribed content. They are not terms for the separate HEC recognized-university, campus or affiliated-college directories and must not be misrepresented as a license for those directories. Conversely, they also do not establish a blanket prohibition or permission for reuse of HEC's institution-directory records; directory-specific terms/permission remain to be established.

Sources:
- https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/DL/Pages/Terms-and-Conditions.aspx
- https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/DL/Pages/Subscription-Process.aspx

## 4. NAVTTC aggregate claims — different categories, no inferred total

NAVTTC's current homepage makes several separate claims, including 650+ accredited institutes and 900+ quality-assured institutes, while its TVET sector overview states 3,500+ TVET institutes across the wider ecosystem. These figures describe different/undefined scopes and cannot be added, substituted or treated as a verified directory count. The staging NAC-TVS directory reviewed earlier remains explicitly representative sample data and is excluded. A production institute search/register with defined scope, last-updated date and record-level status is required before using national counts.

Sources:
- https://navttc.gov.pk/
- https://navttc.gov.pk/tvet-sector/
- https://stagging.navttc.gov.pk/accreditation/institutes

## 5. Rights and data-use clarification

- KPPSRA and Sindh DIRPIS public notices and searchable registers are evidence of public regulatory activity, not automatic permission for bulk extraction or commercial reuse.
- HEC Digital Library publisher licenses apply to subscribed resource content, not automatically to HEC institution directory records.
- NAVTTC homepage aggregate claims require scope definitions and must not be presented as a record-level census.
- No source-specific reuse license or written permission was established in this review. All remain RESEARCH ONLY.
