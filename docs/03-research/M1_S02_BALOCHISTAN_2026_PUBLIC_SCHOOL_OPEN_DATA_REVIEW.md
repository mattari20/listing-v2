# M1-S02 — Balochistan 2026 Public-School Data and Scoped Open-License Review

**Review date:** 2026-11-12  
**Scope:** Official School Education Department of Balochistan data/statistics and reuse policy desk research. No downloads, scraping, record harvesting, source-owner contact or implementation.  
**Status:** Research-only; a narrow explicit reuse permission is documented below, but it does not extend beyond the stated data products.

## 1. Current official source and 2026 data freshness

The Government of Balochistan School Education Department's official site identifies itself as the department's public education record and publishes an analytics dashboard and document centre. The statistics dashboard states that each headline metric carries its source, as-of date, methodology and previous value.

Current source-reported values include:
- **15,368 public schools**, from the SED School List (BEMIS/DDO extract) dated **30 July 2026**.
- **41 districts served**, from the same 30 July 2026 list.
- The methodology says the count includes school rows in the official DDO-code list and excludes **329 administrative DDO rows**.
- **1,245 non-formal education centres**, reported by the Directorate of Non-Formal Education as of **28 February 2026**.
- A **Balochistan Education Statistics 2025–26 Annual School Census** publication dated **30 April 2026** (212 pages), with schools, enrolment, teachers, facilities, district profiles and time series.
- An **Annual School Census 2026–27 data-collection circular** dated **10 June 2026**, indicating the next census collection cycle.

This is materially stronger and fresher than the earlier 2023–24 EMIS statistics PDF, which is labelled DRAFT and reports 15,270 schools. Do not treat the draft 2023–24 figure as the current count or combine it with the 2026 list. The 15,368 figure remains a department-reported public-school aggregate, not an independently audited census by this review.

## 2. Explicit open-data permission — narrow scope

The SED Balochistan statistics dashboard publishes an **Open data policy** stating that headline metrics and district breakdowns are free to reuse with attribution to **"School Education Department, Government of Balochistan"**, citing the as-of date shown with each figure.

This is the first source-specific explicit reuse permission identified in the M1-S02 research. Its scope is limited:
- It applies to the dashboard's headline metrics and district breakdowns as described on that page.
- Attribution and the metric's as-of date are required.
- It does not automatically license the underlying school-level BEMIS/DDO record extract, teacher/payroll data, student-level information, other EMIS pages, private-school records, NFE partner data, photographs, logos or other department content.
- It does not authorize collecting personal/student/teacher records or using authenticated systems.

For M1-S02 rights evidence, classify the dashboard's headline/district aggregates as **OPEN LICENSE — SCOPED** subject to its attribution/date conditions. Keep school-level source records and other SED/EMIS assets **RESEARCH ONLY** until their own terms are verified.

## 3. Data quality and modelling notes

- The dashboard methodology explicitly excludes 329 administrative DDO rows from its 15,368 public-school count. Preserve the school-versus-administrative-office distinction.
- The district count is 41 and includes newly notified districts; district geography and historical time series may not be directly comparable.
- Staff/payroll totals are sourced from a different HR payroll dataset/date (31 March 2026), not the school list. Do not join them into institution profiles.
- NFE centre aggregate (1,245) is from a separate Directorate source dated 28 February 2026; do not assume these are unique school providers or combine them with the formal public-school count.
- The 2023–24 EMIS report is marked DRAFT and reports 15,270; use the 2026 dashboard for current aggregate reference, while retaining the older draft only as historical context.
- The dashboard is an aggregate/district data source. It does not by itself establish an open license for the full institution-level BEMIS/DDO extract.
