## 1. Decision boundary

Finding a public government or regulator page does not establish a right to copy, scrape, store, republish, redistribute or commercialize its contents. This register records observed access routes and the evidence still required. It is not legal advice, a legal opinion or permission to collect data.

**Current default for every source below: RESEARCH ONLY — reuse permission not established.** No scraping, automated collection, connector activation, bulk download, production data population or public republication is authorized by this document.

## 2. Evidence status vocabulary

| Status | Meaning |

## 4. Mandatory review questions for every source

Before a source can move beyond research-only status, record evidence for all applicable questions:

1. **Authority:** Is the publisher the legally competent authority for this exact institution, campus, programme or status?
2. **Ownership:** Who owns or controls the page, dataset and underlying database?
3. **Terms:** Which terms of use, privacy notice, open-data policy, API terms or dataset license apply?
4. **Scope:** Do the terms permit the exact intended activity: manual lookup, caching, storage, normalization, display, attribution, redistribution and commercial use?
5. **Access:** Is there an official API, download, bulk export or written permission? Are authentication, rate limits, robots instructions or anti-automation controls applicable?
6. **Privacy:** Does the source expose personal names, phone numbers, email addresses, precise locations, student-level records or sensitive information? Can V2 use institution-level data only?
7. **Accuracy:** How are corrections, appeals, disputes and stale or withdrawn records handled?
8. **Freshness:** What is the source's publication/update cadence, and can the last-updated date be independently established?
9. **Provenance:** Can V2 retain source URL, observation date, source version, field mapping and verification evidence?
10. **Approval:** Has the owner explicitly approved the proposed use after rights, privacy, security and operational review?

## 5. Minimum evidence packet

A source may be proposed for use only when its evidence packet contains:

- Source owner/authority and official contact route
- Canonical source URL and dated access record
- Copy of applicable terms/license/policy or written permission
- A plain-language summary of permitted and prohibited use
- Exact fields intended to be retained and displayed
- Personal-data screening and minimization decision
- Access method and rate/volume restrictions
- Attribution and source-link requirements
- Update cadence, last-checked date and correction route
- Reviewer, decision date, decision scope and expiry/review date

## 6. Data minimization and special safeguards

- Prefer institution-level information over personal staff or student details.
- Do not collect student names, identity numbers, phone numbers, email addresses, marksheets, exact marks or other unnecessary personal records.
- Do not republish direct staff contact details merely because they appear in a public institute directory.
- Treat disability-related service categories carefully; do not infer or record any individual's disability or health information.
- Do not publish allegations, fake-institute labels, show-cause notices or adverse regulatory statuses without checking current official evidence, context, effective dates and correction process.
- Keep source snapshots and evidence access-controlled if future rights review permits retention; define retention and deletion rules before collection.
