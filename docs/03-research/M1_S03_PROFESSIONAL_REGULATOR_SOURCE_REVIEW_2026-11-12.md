# M1-S03 — Professional Regulator and Programme-Approval Source Review

**Review date:** 2026-11-12  
**Scope:** Official institution/programme source routes for medicine/dentistry, pharmacy, nursing, computing, teacher education, engineering, engineering technology, veterinary education and legal education.  
**Status:** Supplemental research evidence; M1-S03 remains in progress.  
**Independence:** V2 owner requirements and independently consulted official regulator sources only.

## 1. Pakistan Medical and Dental Council (PMDC)

**Official route:** [Institutions and Qualifications](https://pmdc.pk/Colleges).

PMDC's public page separates public medical colleges, public dental colleges, private medical colleges, private dental colleges, foreign undergraduate colleges, Pakistani postgraduate/additional qualifications and foreign postgraduate qualifications. The portal also publishes public alerts, including alerts about unrecognized postgraduate programmes and approved house-job hospitals.

- Undergraduate college recognition, postgraduate qualification recognition, attached-hospital approval, seat allocation and current admission permission are distinct fields/routes.
- A recognized college entry does not automatically prove every programme, campus, intake or postgraduate qualification is currently approved.
- No source-specific data reuse license was verified.

**Disposition:** Official public regulator route observed; **RESEARCH ONLY**.

## 2. Pharmacy Council of Pakistan (PCP)

**Official route:** [Recognized Pharm-D institutions](https://www.pcpisb.gov.pk/institutions?type=Recognized).

The public directory provides institution, province, city, morning/evening seats, programme, status and action fields. It includes **Active** and **Stoppage of Admissions** statuses and separate Recognized and NOC views.

- PCP recognition is programme-specific; "Stoppage of Admissions" must not be converted into a generic institution-closed status.
- Seat allocation and intake mode must be tied to the programme and current session where available.
- An NOC must not silently be treated as full programme recognition.
- No source-specific reuse license was verified.

**Disposition:** Public record-level programme route observed; **RESEARCH ONLY**.

## 3. Pakistan Nursing and Midwifery Council (PNMC)

**Official route:** [Recognized Institutes](https://pnmc.gov.pk/recognized-institutes/).

The page lists nursing/midwifery institutes by name, jurisdiction, city, ownership and programme abbreviations. It also contains a separate fake-institute list and programme labels such as **RN Closed**.

- The page does not present a clear dataset-wide as-of date or current programme approval period.
- Legacy programme labels and inconsistent formatting are present. "RN Closed" is a programme history/status cue, not proof the whole institution is closed.
- Presence in the recognized list is not sufficient to establish that every listed programme is currently accepting students.
- No source-specific reuse license was verified.

**Disposition:** Useful official directory route but freshness and status quality are unresolved; **RESEARCH ONLY**.

## 4. National Computing Education Accreditation Council (NCEAC)

**Official routes:** [Accreditation verification](https://nceac.org.pk/accreditation/accreditInstitutes) and [NCEAC accreditation scope](https://nceac.org.pk/accreditation/NCEAC_Accreditation).

NCEAC explicitly says accreditation applies to specific computing degree programmes, not institutions as a whole. The verification table is searchable/exportable, presents institute names/regions and "Batches" actions, and the reviewed page displayed 410 entries over 41 pages. The page flags the accreditation status of Virtual University and Allama Iqbal Open University as under discussion in the General Council.

- The institution table is a navigation layer; batch/programme detail is necessary to establish accreditation status.
- Preserve programme, batch, campus, decision, visit type, valid period and under-discussion/zero-visit/confirmatory/change-of-scope states where published.
- Do not label an institution globally "accredited" merely because one computing programme or batch is accredited.
- HEC affiliation and NCEAC programme accreditation are separate.
- No source-specific reuse license was verified.

**Disposition:** Public verification route observed; **RESEARCH ONLY**.

## 5. National Accreditation Council for Teacher Education (NACTE)

**Official route:** [Accredited programmes](https://www.nacte.org.pk/Accreditation/AccreditedPrograms).

The public page states that it lists teacher-education programmes holding an active NACTE accreditation award. The observed page displayed 113 programmes and fields for institute, programme, shift, level, province, valid-until date, code and remarks.

- Accreditation is programme-level and time-bounded. Preserve the validity end date and programme code.
- The reviewed page marked several programmes as near expiry, illustrating why an "accredited" badge must be computed from current validity, not historical directory presence.
- The active programme count is a time-sensitive snapshot and must be rechecked before use.
- No source-specific reuse license was verified.

**Disposition:** Strong public programme-level route with explicit validity fields; **RESEARCH ONLY**.

## 6. Pakistan Engineering Council (PEC)

**Official routes:** [Accreditation](https://www.pec.org.pk/accreditation-new/) and [PEC FAQs](https://www.pec.org.pk/faqs/).

PEC describes engineering qualification/programme accreditation as a peer-review process. Its public accreditation section separates Level-I and Level-II programmes, Zero/Interim phase, allowed intake, accreditation procedure and other qualification routes.

- Store programme accreditation, level, intake/batch range and validity/notification separately from institution recognition.
- Level-II and Level-I are not interchangeable; Zero/Interim phase is not equivalent to full accreditation.
- An engineering university may have some accredited programmes and other programmes/intakes with a different status.
- Some PEC public pages were access-limited in this review; record-level extraction and rights are not verified.

**Disposition:** Official scope and register routes identified; some pages access-limited; **RESEARCH ONLY**.

## 7. National Technology Council (NTC)

**Official route:** [NTC programme-scope FAQ](https://registration-ntc.hec.gov.pk/faq).

NTC describes its accreditation and registration purview across engineering technologies, life-science technologies, allied-health-science technologies and applied-physics technologies.

- Preserve NTC versus PEC authority and programme family; do not infer that an NTC technology programme is a PEC engineering programme.
- Registration/accreditation must be checked at programme and relevant intake/batch level.
- The FAQ defines scope but is not itself a complete current accredited-institute register.

**Disposition:** Official scope evidence observed; complete current programme register and rights remain to be verified; **RESEARCH ONLY**.

## 8. Pakistan Veterinary Medical Council (PVMC)

**Official route:** [Accredited/recognized institutions](https://www.pvmc.gov.pk/Detail/Y2I2YzBjMDItNTRiZS00NzE0LThhODYtMmQyM2U2ZWNkN2U0).

The public page presents institution names, accredited programmes and accreditation periods. The same page includes institutional officer contact details.

- Accreditation period and programme are essential; institution-wide status cannot be inferred from a single programme row.
- Exclude personal contact names and direct phone numbers from V2's public provider data.
- The page contains historical programme names and periods; verify current status before any present-tense approval claim.
- No source-specific reuse license was verified.

**Disposition:** Official programme/accreditation route observed; historical/current status and rights require verification; **RESEARCH ONLY**.

## 9. Pakistan Bar Council (PBC) — legal education

**Official route:** [Recognized affiliated colleges](https://www.pakistanbarcouncil.org/legal-education/affiliated_colleges).

The page lists law colleges grouped by affiliating university, with place, affiliation, recognition date and a "Student Fees" field containing annual Paid/Unpaid values.

- PBC legal-education recognition, university affiliation and HEC university recognition are separate relationships.
- The "Student Fees" heading and Paid/Unpaid values must not be interpreted as tuition fees without a data dictionary; do not present them as student cost.
- Recognition date alone does not prove current admission permission, active programme delivery or current affiliation continuation.
- No source-specific reuse license was verified.

**Disposition:** Public affiliated-college route observed; field semantics and current continuation require verification; **RESEARCH ONLY**.

## 10. Cross-regulator modelling and quality rules

1. Separate institution, campus, programme, intake/batch, awarding body, regulator and affiliation records.
2. Preserve exact authority status and scope; do not normalize "Active", "Stoppage of Admissions", "RN Closed", "Under Discussion", "Zero Visit", "Interim", "Level-I", "Level-II", "Near to Expire" or "Unrecognized" into one generic status.
3. Store source date, as-of date, programme code, intake/batch, valid-from/to and notification where available.
4. Treat a regulator's public directory as an evidence source, not a blanket right to reuse its content.
5. Exclude personal contacts, student details, registration identifiers and other unnecessary personal data.
6. All sources remain **RESEARCH ONLY**. No scraping, bulk download, API activation, source-owner contact or permission request was performed.
