# M1-S03 — AJK Education Department and Higher Education Source Review

**Review date:** 2026-11-12  
**Scope:** Azad Jammu & Kashmir Elementary & Secondary Education (E&SE), EMIS/private-school route, and Higher Education Department (HED) public college sources.  
**Status:** Supplemental official-source research; M1-S03 remains in progress.

## 1. Elementary & Secondary Education Department (E&SE)

**Official route:** [E&SE Department portal](https://ese.gok.pk/), [News and Notifications](https://www.ese.gok.pk/news-and-notifications), [EMIS portal](https://emis.ese.gok.pk/), [PEIRA private-institution portal](https://peira.ese.gok.pk/).

The E&SE homepage identifies a Directorate of EMIS and provides an official link to the private-school regulator/PEIRA and the EMIS portal. Its public dashboard currently displays 6,019 total schools, 31,795 teachers, 506,851 enrolled students and a gross-enrolment-rate figure of 89.7%. The same homepage's "Quick Overview" displays zero for literacy and girls'/boys' intake-rate cards.

**Data-quality and access findings**
- The 6,019 school figure is a department homepage aggregate; no clear as-of date or record-level public school register was established from the homepage.
- Zero-valued quick-overview cards conflict with other non-zero dashboard values and should be treated as unpopulated/failed metrics, not actual zero outcomes.
- The EMIS portal presents a username/password login; no public unauthenticated school-level dataset was verified.
- The department's news page records an official announcement dated 5 November 2025 for a "List of Registered Private Educational Institutes in AJ&K" and a 2024–25 private-institution census guideline. The private-institution list link opens a Google Drive file that prompts sign-in in the public review. The PEIRA portal is an authenticated provider-update route, not a public directory.
- The E&SE site links to a 2019–20 Annual Census Report. This is a historical source, not current 2026 school-level evidence.
- Do not use the homepage's teacher or enrolled-student aggregate to create individual teacher/student data or infer school-level completeness.

**Assessment:** Official E&SE authority and current aggregate dashboard observed; school-level public access remains unverified; private list is announced but not publicly readable in this review. **PARTIAL / RESEARCH ONLY**.

## 2. Higher Education Department (HED)

**Official routes:** [HED functions](https://hed.ajk.gov.pk/funds/), [HED overview](https://hed.ajk.gov.pk/hed-overview/), [Degree Colleges](https://hed.ajk.gov.pk/degree-colleges/), [Inter Colleges](https://hed.ajk.gov.pk/inter-colleges/), [Educational Colleges](https://hed.ajk.gov.pk/educational-colleges/).

HED's functions page states that it manages 180 colleges across 10 districts and supervises one BISE, five public universities and three autonomous higher-education/professional institutions. A separate HED overview page states that it manages 178 colleges through its field directorate network. HED publishes separate degree-college, inter-college and education-college lists.

**Data-quality and scope findings**
- The official 180 versus 178 aggregate conflict remains unresolved. It may reflect differing scope, date or category coverage; do not average the totals or select one as definitive.
- The category-specific lists are useful institution source routes but do not, by themselves, reconcile the aggregate or establish all private-sector coverage.
- College, inter-college and teacher-education college types must remain distinguishable; deduplicate campus/college variants and preserve institution type.
- The lists do not consistently expose a dataset-wide as-of date, current operational status, current affiliation or source reuse terms.
- HED public lists are not a substitute for HEC recognition, professional programme accreditation or board affiliation verification.

**Assessment:** Official HED mandate and multiple public college lists observed; aggregate count and completeness remain unresolved. **RESEARCH ONLY**.

## 4. V2 handling

- Keep public-sector school, private registered school, inter college, degree college, education/teacher-training college and university as distinct provider types.
- Preserve source as-of date, source identifier, jurisdiction, category and access class.
- Do not infer private-school completeness from a dated announcement or a login-only portal.
- Do not infer school-level completeness from the 6,019 aggregate.
- Do not combine HED's 178 and 180 totals without a scope/date reconciliation.
- No scraping, download, login, access bypass, contact, permission request or source reuse was performed.
