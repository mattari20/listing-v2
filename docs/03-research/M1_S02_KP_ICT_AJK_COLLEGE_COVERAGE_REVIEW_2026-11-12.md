# M1-S02 — KP Public-School EMIS, ICT Federal Schools and AJK College Coverage Review

**Review date:** 2026-11-12  
**Scope:** Official public-source desk research only. No scraping, downloads, personal-data collection, external contact, permission requests or implementation.  
**Status:** Research-only; no source reuse or S02 acceptance is implied.

## 1. Khyber Pakhtunkhwa — public-school iEMIS is role-based, not a public directory

KP Elementary & Secondary Education Department's 30 May 2026 iEMIS notice states that its integrated Education Management Information System connects attached formations and is role-based, with permissions granted to users according to job responsibilities/domain. The department's site also advertises a School Self Reporting portal for school-level data collection and provides a portal link to iEMIS. This establishes a current official public-school data system but does not establish that a public, unauthenticated school-level directory or downloadable public dataset is available.

KPPSRA remains a separate private-school regulator/register. Do not combine its private-school records with the role-based public-school iEMIS without a lawful/public access route and a clear scope definition. The public-source gap for a complete, current KP government-school register remains open.

Sources:
- https://kpese.gov.pk/integrated-education-management-information-system-iemis/
- https://kpese.gov.pk/
- https://www.psra.gkp.pk/registers

## 2. Islamabad Capital Territory — FDE public-sector school/college aggregate

The Federal Directorate of Education's current Education Transformation Portal reports **432 FDE institutions** and presents an integrated programme/support portal for schools and colleges. This is a department-level aggregate and institutional support portal, not a record-level public school directory. It is useful evidence of the FDE public-sector institutional network but cannot establish school names, individual locations, levels, campus relationships or complete coverage. ICT-PEIRA remains the separate private-school registration regulator. A current public FDE institution list or approved open dataset remains to be verified.

Sources:
- https://www.fde.edu.pk/
- https://peira.gov.pk/

## 3. AJK — higher-education college lists and conflicting aggregate totals

AJK Higher Education Department publishes separate lists for degree colleges, intermediate colleges, postgraduate colleges, model science colleges and educational/teacher-training colleges. This provides a much stronger public-sector college discovery source than an aggregate-only page, but categories may overlap (for example, a college can appear in more than one level/type list) and the pages do not by themselves establish current operating/affiliation status or private-college coverage.

Two official HED pages publish different aggregate totals:
- Overview page: **178 colleges** managed through 10 district directorates.
- Functions page: **180 colleges** managed across 10 districts.

This two-college difference is unresolved. Do not select either as a verified current total or sum the separate category lists. AJK HED also states that it supervises one BISE, five public universities and three autonomous higher-education institutions; these relationships should be cross-checked with the board/university's own recognition sources.

- https://hed.ajk.gov.pk/degree-colleges/
- https://hed.ajk.gov.pk/inter-colleges/

## 4. Data quality, rights and modelling implications

- A role-based EMIS is not equivalent to a public directory; access must not be bypassed.
- Department-level counts are not record-level inventories.
- AJK's 178/180 discrepancy must be resolved with a dated official explanation before using either as a count.
- AJK college categories may overlap; deduplicate by a source-supported institution identifier and retain category/level relationships.
- FDE's 432 institution figure must not be represented as 432 individually verified schools or colleges.
- Public-facing pages do not establish permission for bulk collection, republication or commercial use. No source-specific license or written permission was established.
- Do not request, store or expose student, teacher or staff personal data from role-based EMIS systems.
