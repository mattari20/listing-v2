## 1. Examination and qualification-board ecosystem

### IBCC Forum Members
IBCC publishes a forum-members list separating Boards of Secondary Education, Technical Boards and Other Boards. The surfaced list includes provincial/territorial BISE members, FBISE, technical boards such as PBTE, SBTE and KP-BT&CE, and other examining bodies such as AIOU and AKU-EB. This is useful as an authority/board discovery and relationship map, not as an institution directory. It should inform the taxonomy's examining-board and awarding-body entities and the institution-to-board affiliation relationship. The list contains repeated entries and abbreviated names in the retrieved rendering, so exact board identities and current membership should be normalized against each board's own official site before use.

Source:
- https://ibcc.edu.pk/ibcc-forum-members/

### Punjab BISE network
Punjab Higher Education Department states that nine Boards of Intermediate and Secondary Education serve public and private sector intermediate colleges across Punjab: Bahawalpur, D.G. Khan, Faisalabad, Gujranwala, Lahore, Multan, Rawalpindi, Sahiwal and Sargodha. These boards are examination/assessment authorities, not institution owners or quality-accreditation bodies. An institution may have multiple affiliations/examination relationships; board jurisdiction must not be treated as ownership or general institutional recognition.

Source:
- https://hed.punjab.gov.pk/boards

### FBISE jurisdiction caution
FBISE's published brochure describes a jurisdiction extending beyond ICT, including specified cantonment-board institutions, territories outside provinces and Pakistani schools abroad. The brochure is old (2018-era publication) and should not be used as a definitive 2026 jurisdiction statement without current Act/rules confirmation. It nevertheless demonstrates why board jurisdiction is not a simple province-to-board one-to-one mapping.

Source:
- https://www.fbise.edu.pk/Downloads/BROCHURE-31.pdf

## 2. Punjab Medical Faculty — allied-health/paramedical affiliation source

Punjab Medical Faculty publishes separate affiliated-institute lists for private institutions, public institutions under the Specialized Healthcare & Medical Education Department, and public institutions outside that department. This is a distinct affiliation/recognition source family from university/HEC recognition and from professional registration of an individual practitioner. The current list page is a source lead; programme, campus, intake, affiliation validity, status and latest update must be verified in the underlying category lists before individual claims are made.

Source:
- https://pmfpunjab.edu.pk/PMF/AffiliationDetail

## 3. DGRE jurisdiction and registered-Madaris coverage — deeper validation

DGRE's jurisdiction page enumerates its 16 regional offices and the districts assigned across Punjab, Balochistan, KP, Sindh, AJK and GB. The public regional Madaris lists located in this review are dated **26 March 2024** and include Islamabad, Sindh, KP, Balochistan, AJK and GB. This confirms that the earlier Islamabad/Sindh-only observation was incomplete: DGRE has region-specific lists across multiple territories. It does not establish a complete current national 2026 register.

The surfaced lists contain data-quality issues that make record-level verification essential:
- The KP list includes at least one row whose district/address text refers to Lahore/Punjab.
- The Balochistan list includes at least one entry whose address text ends in another province and contains blank/incomplete institution-name cells in the displayed records.
- The AJK/GB/Balochistan pages preserve the same 2024 publication date, not a 2026 verification date.
- The lists expose a maslak field; exclude it from V2 general institution discovery.
- The 16-office jurisdiction table contains spelling/administrative inconsistencies and should not be treated as a normalized authoritative district crosswalk without source-owner confirmation.

DGRE also describes its registration workflow through regional offices and annual educational-activity reporting. That process description is not evidence that a specific record remains active today. DGRE's nationwide registration objective should not be mistaken for proof that all madaris are registered or that the published pages are complete.

## 4. Rights, privacy and status controls

- A board membership list does not prove an institution's affiliation; verify institution-specific affiliation and current validity.
- A BISE relationship is an examination/assessment relationship, not an accreditation or ownership relationship.
- Punjab Medical Faculty affiliation is not the same as HEC recognition or individual professional registration.
- DGRE regional lists dated 2024 require current freshness checks. Record-level address/region anomalies must be resolved, not silently normalized.
- Exclude personal contacts and DGRE maslak/sect data from general V2 institution profiles.
- Public availability is not a license for collection, republication or commercial reuse. No source-specific license or written permission was established. All sources remain RESEARCH ONLY.

## 6. HEC affiliated-college and study-centre policy — important 2024 clarification

HEC's Institutional Affiliation Policy 2024 states that continuation of affiliation for public-sector university/DAI affiliated colleges is to be notified by the respective affiliating DAI after the HEC desk-review process. The policy also applies to approved study centres where regular classes are conducted and centres operated under MoUs with public/private institutions. HEC's Accreditation Section separately lists recognized affiliated colleges of public-sector HEIs, recognized campuses, NOCs, fake/illegal/unrecognized institutions and unaccredited institutions. These must be separate evidence/status categories.

The HEC recognized-affiliate-college PDF is extensive (the surfaced document is 158 pages) and the retrieved rows contain apparent duplicates and spelling/punctuation variants. A listed affiliate-college relationship must therefore be tied to the affiliating university/DAI, the relevant programme, current continuation notification, campus/study-centre identity and effective period. A parent university's HEC recognition does not by itself prove that every affiliated college or study centre has current approval.

Sources:
- https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/universities/Documents/AFFILIATION%20CRITERIA.pdf
- https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/universities/Pages/Accreditation.aspx
- https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/universities/Documents/List%20of%20the%20Recognized%20Affiliate%20College%20of%20Public%20Sector-HEIs.pdf

## 7. Current programme-status examples in health education

PM&DC's current public/private medical-college lists include seat allocations and explicit court-order notes permitting admissions for specified 2026–27 sessions at particular institutions. PCP's Pharm-D list separately uses statuses such as Active and Stoppage of Admissions. These examples confirm that institution presence, programme approval, seat allocation, admission permission and admission stoppage are distinct facts. The applicable session and legal/authority basis must be retained; a generic active/inactive institution flag is inadequate.

Sources:
- https://pmdc.pk/Colleges/PublicMedicalColleges
- https://www.pmdc.pk/Colleges/PrivateMedicalColleges
- https://www.pcpisb.gov.pk/institutions?type=Recognized

## 8. Nursing register freshness warning

PNMC's recognized-institutes page exposes institution, province, city, sector and programme details, but the search-indexed record sequence reaches at least 608 entries while the page's search freshness metadata is old. Some programme cells contain closed/irregular entries. Treat it as a source-family lead only until the council confirms the current register version, active programme/intake scope and update date. Do not infer current approval solely from a row's presence.
