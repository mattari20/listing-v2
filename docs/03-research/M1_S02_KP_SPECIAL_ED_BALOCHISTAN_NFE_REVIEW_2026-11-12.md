# M1-S02 — KP Special-Education and Balochistan NFE Register Deep Review

**Review date:** 2026-11-12  
**Scope:** Official public-source desk research only. No scraping, data collection, student-record access, external contact, permission request or implementation.  
**Status:** Research-only; this does not approve reuse or close M1-S02.

## 1. Khyber Pakhtunkhwa special education — official centre list lead

The KP Zakat, Ushr, Social Welfare, Special Education & Women Empowerment Department publishes a page titled "Mentally Retarded & Physically Handicapped Childrens" with a table of 15 named centres/institutions, education level and enrolment aggregates. The table explicitly marks Govt School for MR & PH Malakand as **NON FUNCTIONAL** with zero enrolment. This is a materially stronger current official facility lead than the historical parliamentary document previously identified.

However:
- The page does not expose a clear publication/update date in the retrieved view.
- The table mixes centre names, service/organization names and enrolment statistics.
- One status is explicitly non-functional; the other records should not automatically be assumed active solely because a row exists.
- Enrolment counts are learner-level aggregate data and are not required for institution discovery; do not copy them into the V2 institution source register.
- Do not collect or expose any individual child identity, disability/diagnosis, health or therapy information.

Treat this as a partial KP special-education source family with 15 listed centre/provider entries requiring current operating-status and district-coverage verification, not as a complete KP inventory.

Source:
- https://www.swkpk.gov.pk/mentally-retarded-students.php

## 2. Current KP special-education activity — proposals versus existing facilities

KP government news reports in August and September 2026 describe visits to existing social-welfare/special-education field formations, including a Centre for Children with Mental and Physical Disabilities in Lower Chitral and a Deaf School in Peshawar. A separate September 2026 report describes proposed new special-welfare complexes for Khyber and Bajaur, which must be treated as proposed infrastructure rather than existing operational centres. These reports corroborate current service activity but do not provide a complete institution register.

## 3. Balochistan non-formal education — organization-level centre data

Balochistan EMIS exposes a dedicated Non-Formal Education page with an organization-wise table of centres, teachers and learners. The displayed organizations include the Directorate of Literacy & Non-Formal Education Balochistan, BECS, NCHD, NRSP, BRSP, IRC/TEACH, UNICEF-supported programmes and other partners. This confirms a richer NFE programme/provider ecosystem than a navigation link alone.

The page does not provide a clearly visible reporting date or a record-level public list of each centre in the retrieved view. Some displayed counts are unusual or duplicated across fields (for example, several rows repeat centre totals in separate columns), and partner/programme names may represent funded delivery programmes rather than distinct legal provider institutions. Do not add the organization counts together or treat them as unique current centre totals without the data dictionary, reporting period, programme scope and source-owner clarification. Keep organization, programme, implementing partner and individual centre as separate entities.

Source:
- https://emis.gob.pk/NF/NonFormalHome.aspx

## 4. Data minimization and quality requirements

- Record the existence of KP centre names, location/district if published, service category and explicit operational status only after verification.
- Exclude enrolment counts, child profiles, disability/diagnosis, medical/therapy data and staff/teacher personal details from the institution discovery dataset.
- Do not treat a planned complex as an operating institution.
- Do not assume rows with no explicit status are active; use "status not stated / needs verification".
- Balochistan NFE table is organization/programme-level aggregate evidence, not a record-level centre register.
- No source-specific reuse license or written permission was established. All remain RESEARCH ONLY.
