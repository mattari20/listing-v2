# M1-S04 — Private Provider and Institution-Source Research

**Status:** Initial independent private-provider source inventory and gap analysis completed; prepared for owner acceptance. This is a research baseline, not a complete national census, legal opinion, permission grant or authorization to collect/republish data.  
**Prepared / checked:** 2026-11-12  
**Repository:** `mattari20/listing-v2`  
**Independence:** Research performed independently for Listing V2; no legacy repository artifacts, datasets, schemas, implementation patterns or historical findings used.

## 1. Purpose and boundaries

M1-S04 identifies non-government education providers and source routes that may support future V2 institution discovery. It complements M1-S02 taxonomy and M1-S03 official government/regulator source research by concentrating on private, privately managed, non-state, NGO/community and provider-operated source families.

This register distinguishes:
- the provider and its operating campus/site;
- a regulator's registration or licensing status;
- a university's charter/recognition and campus status;
- a board, professional council or awarding body's programme/session approval;
- accreditation or quality-assurance status;
- provider-submitted descriptions and marketing claims.

These are separate facts. One must never be inferred from another.

**Research-only boundary:** Public visibility of a page or registry does not grant permission to scrape, bulk-download, store, republish, commercialize or otherwise reuse its records. All sources below remain **RESEARCH ONLY** pending source-specific terms, rights, privacy and technical-access review. No source-owner contact, permission request, scraping, bulk download, connector activation or ingestion was performed or authorized.

## 4. Data model and verification implications

1. **Provider, campus and branch:** one operator may manage multiple institutions or campuses; one name can represent several separate legal entities. Keep parent/operator, institution and physical campus/site distinct.
2. **Registration versus recognition:** school registration, HERA/PEIRA registration, HEC charter/recognition, university affiliation, board registration, professional approval and accreditation are separate assertions with separate issuing authorities and effective periods.
3. **Status lifecycle:** preserve source-native labels and dates (e.g. active, renewal pending, renewal awaited, unregistered, blocked, dormant, closed, de-registered, admissions stopped). Do not normalize them into a single "recognized" boolean without documented mapping and authority.
4. **Programme and session scope:** institution-level registration does not prove every programme, campus, intake, qualification or examination session is approved.
5. **Evidence provenance:** each material claim needs source identity, source URL, observation/as-of date, applicable jurisdiction, source-native status, verification method and confidence.
6. **Provider-submitted content:** label it as provider-submitted until independently corroborated; marketing claims cannot establish legal status or quality.
7. **Privacy:** public registries may expose owners' names, personal phone numbers, emails or other individual details. Do not replicate personal contacts by default. Prefer institution-level public contact channels; require a documented lawful basis, necessity and retention rule for any personal data.
8. **Duplicates and historical records:** repeated campuses, spelling variants, aliases, expired registrations and branch moves require identity reconciliation. Do not merge records on name similarity alone.
9. **Deeni Madaris:** retain the distinct underlying category and V2-DEC-006 default-OFF public visibility rule. Do not add sect/maslak discovery attributes.
10. **Non-state provider type:** distinguish private commercial, not-for-profit/NGO, community, faith-based, franchise/chain and online-only operating models without implying that legal form confers education recognition.

## 7. Evidence links and observation date

All URLs above were checked as public source routes on 2026-11-12. Principal source observations:
- Punjab PEPRIS describes registration, renewal and the valid e-license parent-corner list.
- KP-PSRA presents district-selectable private-school records with source-native registration status.
- ICT-PEIRA describes the regulator's HSSC-level mandate and active/renewal status categories.
- AJK E&SE links its private-institution registration portal and a registered-institutes list; a department notice dates the list to 2025-11-05.
- Sindh SELD documents DIRPIS and private-school census work; the private-college page's visible record dates are historical and require current confirmation.
- Balochistan SED describes BPEIRRA's legal mandate and EMIS includes a Private Schools route.
- HEC publishes separate university/DAI and campus recognition pages.
- Punjab HED publishes private-college district links and a private-university list.
- KP HERA publishes a private-college register and verification categories; homepage and category-page counters require reconciliation.
- NAVTTC provides an institute registration portal and distinguishes provider registration from NAC-TVS accreditation; staging/sample directory data is not treated as authoritative.
- Regional TEVTA portals are separate provider-source families and need record-level public/private classification.

**Conclusion:** M1-S04 establishes a credible first map of private-provider source families and identifies significant jurisdictional and sector gaps. It does not establish a complete private-provider census, current operation of each listed provider, blanket recognition, legal reuse rights or permission to collect data.
