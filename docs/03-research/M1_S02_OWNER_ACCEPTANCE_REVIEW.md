## 3. Evidence-backed conclusions

- Pakistan's provider landscape is multi-sector and multi-jurisdictional; a single national directory does not cover every provider family.
- Provider, campus, branch/study centre, programme, regulator, registration, affiliation, examination/assessment, awarding and quality-accreditation relationships must remain separate.
- Professional approvals often attach to a programme, campus, shift, intake or session rather than an entire institution. NCEAC explicitly describes programme-specific accreditation; PEC, NACTE and NBEAC publish programme/session/level/validity details.
- School coverage requires distinct public, private, community, non-formal and special-education source families. Punjab public OpenPunjab and private PEPRIS are separate; KPPSRA exposes district-level private-school statuses; Sindh ASC has annual profiles; Balochistan EMIS separates public, private and NFE areas.
- TVET provider registration, QAB affiliation, assessment/certification and NAC-TVS quality accreditation are separate.
- Source data quality varies. PNMC page freshness/formatting, PCP geography anomalies, NCH closed entries/personal contacts, and staging/test TVET sample content require caution.
- Public visibility does not grant rights to scrape, store, normalize, republish or commercialize directory records. No source-specific reuse license or written permission has been established in this review.

## 4. Unresolved gaps and carried-forward risks

1. Record-by-record verification and freshness checks are not complete; the research is a source-family baseline, not a national institution census.
2. Public/private and district coverage is not proven complete for AJK, GB and some other jurisdictions/sectors.
3. Some professional councils have been identified through HEC's council map, but current public institution/programme registers and status semantics remain unverified for every discipline.
4. Source-specific terms, licenses, permitted access methods, attribution, caching, correction and commercial-use rights remain unresolved for multiple agencies.
5. No written permission requests or source-owner/legal contacts were made.
6. Privacy review is a research-level minimization assessment, not a formal legal/privacy approval.
7. All candidate sources remain RESEARCH ONLY. No source is approved for manual verification, bulk collection, ingestion, republication or commercial use.
