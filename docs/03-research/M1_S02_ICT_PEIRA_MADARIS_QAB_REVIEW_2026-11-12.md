## 1. ICT private educational institutions — PEIRA

ICT-PEIRA states that private educational institutions operating in ICT up to higher-secondary level or equivalent fall under its statutory registration/regulation domain. Its public site describes listed PEIs as active and validly registered and says the list is updated every weekend pending an auto-update system. It separately explains renewal-in-process, renewal-awaited and unregistered states. These statuses must not be collapsed.

The linked public register is paginated (the retrieved page shows 70 pages) and displays EMIS number, institution name, address, sector/area and educational level. PEIRA says records are provided by PEIs at registration/renewal and the concerned PEI is responsible for discrepancies. This is useful ICT private-school discovery and registration evidence, but not independently verified proof of every profile claim. Registration is distinct from board affiliation, examination recognition, programme accreditation and academic quality. Use the 2024 ICT-PEIRA Rules as the governing rules reference.

## 2. Deeni Madaris — separate provider family and registration authority

The Directorate General of Religious Education (DGRE), under the Ministry of Federal Education & Professional Training, states that its mandate includes registering Deeni Madaris nationwide and maintaining relevant data. It describes 16 regional offices, including Lahore, Multan, Rawalpindi/Islamabad, Karachi, Sukkur, Hyderabad, Peshawar, D.I. Khan, Swat, Quetta, Loralai, Khuzdar, Muzaffarabad, Mirpur, Gilgit and Skardu. DGRE public pages include region-specific registered-madaris lists; surfaced Islamabad and Sindh pages are explicitly dated 26 March 2024. These are useful official leads but cannot be represented as a complete current 2026 national register without newer freshness confirmation.

For taxonomy, Deeni Madaris should be a distinct provider family with a registration relationship to DGRE and possible separate examination/awarding-body relationships. Do not collapse DGRE registration into IBCC equivalence, board affiliation, university recognition or professional accreditation. DGRE's public list includes a maslak field; this is unnecessary for general institution discovery and is excluded from V2 attributes and filters.

## 3. IBCC Qualification Awarding Bodies — recognition is not equivalence

IBCC publishes recognized Qualification Awarding Bodies, including foreign examination bodies, indigenous boards/schools and named Deeni Madaris/wafaqs. IBCC explicitly cautions that recognition of a QAB does not mean all qualifications offered by it are automatically recognized for equivalence. Its equivalence FAQ separately lists recognized Deeni Madaris/wafaqs and explains that equivalence of particular religious certificates is subject to verification and additional-subject conditions.

Keep four distinct relationships:
- **Provider registration:** an institution is registered with its relevant regulator (e.g. DGRE).
- **Examining/awarding-body recognition:** a board/body is recognized for specified examining/certification functions.
- **Qualification equivalence:** a particular credential may be accepted as equivalent to a national qualification, subject to conditions.
- **Institution recognition/accreditation:** a separate authority decision concerning an institution, campus or programme.

Do not infer an individual madrasa's recognition from affiliation to a listed wafaq; do not infer every credential's equivalence from recognition of a QAB. Verify institution, awarding body, specific qualification, conditions and effective date separately.

Sources:
- https://ibcc.edu.pk/qualification-awarding-bodies/
- https://ibcc.edu.pk/faqs/equivalence-faqs/

## 4. Rights, privacy and minimization

- PEIRA's register is public and states that records are submitted by PEIs; this is not a license for automated extraction, republication or commercial reuse.
- DGRE public lists contain institution names, locations and a maslak field. Exclude maslak/sect data from general institution discovery because it is unnecessary to the provider directory.
- Do not collect personal identifiers, individuals' religious beliefs, student records, staff profiles, personal phone numbers or private contacts.
- Public lists dated 2024 must carry publication dates and stale/unverified freshness status until a newer official source confirms them.
- No source-specific license or written permission for automated collection, storage, republication or commercial reuse was established. All sources remain RESEARCH ONLY.
