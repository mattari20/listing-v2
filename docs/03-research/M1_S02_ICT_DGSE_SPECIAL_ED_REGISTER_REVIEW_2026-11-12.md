# M1-S02 — ICT Directorate General of Special Education Register Review

**Review date:** 2026-11-12  
**Scope:** Official DGSE and Ministry of Human Rights public institutional pages. No admission forms, student records, personal data, health/disability records, downloads, external contacts or implementation.  
**Status:** Research-only; no source reuse permission or S02 acceptance is implied.

## 1. Current ICT special-education provider evidence

The Directorate General of Special Education (DGSE), Ministry of Federal Education & Professional Training, publishes an official "Admission Policy of Centres/Institutions" page naming four centre-level education providers:
1. National Special Education Centre for Hearing Impaired Children, H-9/4, Islamabad.
2. Al-Farabi National Special Education Centre for Physically Handicapped Children, G-8/4, Islamabad.
3. Al-Maktoom National Special Education Centre for Visually Handicapped Children, G-7/2, Islamabad.
4. Rehabilitation Centre for Children with Developmental Disorder, H-8/4, Islamabad.

The Ministry of Human Rights' "Present Picture of DGSE" page lists 11 institutions/units currently working under DGSE in Islamabad, including the four education/rehabilitation centres above, National Training Centre for Special Persons, National Institute of Special Education, National Braille Press, National Library and Resource Centre, National Mobility and Independence Training Centre, Vocational Rehabilitation and Employment of Disabled Persons and Resource Unit for Autistic Children.

The 11-item list mixes education centres, training institutes, a press, library/resource unit, mobility/independence training and vocational rehabilitation services. Do not represent all 11 as schools. Model each entity according to its function/provider type. The National Assembly response states DGSE's jurisdiction is restricted to ICT; do not treat this directorate's list as a national special-education directory.

## 2. Freshness and status

DGSE's official site has an admissions announcement for the 2025–26 session and individual centre profiles. This is stronger evidence of current operation than a mandate-only page, but the Ministry of Human Rights' 11-unit list has no clearly stated last-verified date in the retrieved page. The centre-specific DGSE pages and session announcement should be used as separate evidence sources; the current operating status and scope of each non-school service unit should be checked before listing as a school.

## 3. Privacy and data minimization

DGSE pages include child admission criteria and may request guardian CNIC, child Form-B, disability certificates, medical assessments and other sensitive information. These are admissions/eligibility records, not institution-discovery attributes. Do not collect or publish:
- student/child names, Form-B, guardian CNIC or contact details;
- disability diagnoses, medical/therapy records or health screening;
- individual student counts or profiles as institution attributes;
- staff/principal personal contact details.

For institution discovery, retain only institution name, public institutional address, institution type/service category, official institutional contact channel if separately justified, source, source date and status. No reuse license or written permission for automated collection or republication was established.

## 4. Effect on M1-S02

- **S02-C06:** ICT special education is upgraded from mandate-only to **partial official institution list**, with four education/rehabilitation centres and 11 mixed service units identified. The latter must not all be classified as schools.
