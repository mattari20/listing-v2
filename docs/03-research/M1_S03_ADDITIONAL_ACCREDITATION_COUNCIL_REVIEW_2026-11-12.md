# M1-S03 — Additional Accreditation Council and Professional Register Review

**Review date:** 2026-11-12  
**Scope:** HEC accreditation-council authority map, NBEAC, NCH, PCATP, AHPC and NAEAC official source routes.  
**Status:** Supplemental research evidence; M1-S03 remains in progress.

## 1. HEC Quality Assurance Agency — accreditation council map

**Official route:** [HEC Accreditation Councils](https://www.hec.gov.pk/urdu/services/universities/QAA/Pages/Accreditation-Councils.aspx).

HEC's Quality Assurance Agency lists professional bodies/councils and HEC-established accreditation councils, including PBC, PCATP, PMDC/legacy PMC naming, PEC, PNMC/legacy PNC naming, PCP, PVMC, NCH, NCT, NACTE, NAEAC, NCEAC, NBEAC and NTC.

**Interpretation:** This is a federal coordination/authority map, not a live list of approved institutions or programmes. Preserve the legal/current name and statute of each council; do not assume the HEC page itself confers accreditation or recognition.

## 2. National Business Education Accreditation Council (NBEAC)

**Official route:** [Accredited Business Schools](https://nbeac.org.pk/index.php/accredited-business-schools).

The page separates business schools by accreditation category (including W and X categories) and provides institution, department, programmes, session, sector, chartered region and status. The reviewed list includes statuses **Accredited**, **Reaccreditation**, **Under Process** and **Expired**, with sessions ranging across 2014–2031.

**V2 interpretation**
- NBEAC approval is programme/department/school-specific and time-bounded, not a university-wide accreditation.
- Preserve the exact category, department, programmes, session, sector, region and status.
- A status of Under Process or Reaccreditation must not be presented as current full accreditation; Expired must remain distinct.
- The public list contains historical and current session rows and may include typographical inconsistencies in region fields. Verify the exact row/session before a student-facing claim.
- No source-specific reuse license was verified.

**Disposition:** Strong public programme/department-level register; **RESEARCH ONLY**.

## 3. National Council for Homoeopathy (NCH)

**Official route:** [Recognized Homoeopathic Medical Colleges](https://www.nchpakistan.gov.pk/Colleges.html).

The public page is titled "List of Recognised Homoeopathic Medical Colleges" and lists institution names, addresses and principal contact details.

**Data-quality/privacy findings**
- The page does not show a clear as-of date, recognition validity period, programme/session status or source data dictionary.
- It publishes principal names and direct telephone/mobile numbers. Those personal contact fields are excluded from V2's provider register and must not be copied.
- Recognition of a college should not be generalized to every programme, campus or intake without separate evidence.
- No source-specific reuse license was verified.

**Disposition:** Official recognized-college source route observed; freshness and status semantics remain limited; **RESEARCH ONLY**.

## 4. Pakistan Council of Architects and Town Planners (PCATP)

**Official routes:** [Accredited Programmes](https://pcatp.org.pk/accredited-programs.php), [Academic/Accreditation notices](https://pcatp.org.pk/academic.php), [PCATP portal](https://pcatp.org.pk/).

PCATP's public portal links to accreditation status, professional institutions, approved architects and town planners, registered firms and examination/registration functions. Search-indexed PCATP material includes an older programme document identifying architecture programmes running/closed without PCATP NOC/approval.

**V2 interpretation**
- Separate programme accreditation/NOC, professional registration of individuals, firm registration and institution recognition.
- The older programme document is historical and cannot establish current 2026 programme status.
- The public page did not provide a clean, current, fully dated institution/programme register in this review.
- Do not infer that professional architect registration means the individual's education provider or every programme is currently accredited.
- No source-specific reuse license was verified.

**Disposition:** Official authority and accreditation routes identified; current register freshness incomplete; **PARTIAL / RESEARCH ONLY**.

## 5. Allied Health Professionals Council (AHPC)

**Official route:** [List of institutions applied for recognition/accreditation (PDF, 16-Nov-2025)](https://www.ahpc.org.pk/assets/final-list-of-institutes-16-11-25.pdf).

The official PDF is explicitly titled as a list of institutions **applied for** recognition/accreditation. It contains a large multi-province list of applicant institutions.

**Critical status distinction**
- Application is not approval, recognition granted, accreditation, NOC or permission to admit.
- Do not use the applicant list as an approved-institute directory or present listed institutions as recognized by AHPC.
- A separate current public register of granted recognition/approved programmes was not verified in this review.
- No source-specific reuse license was verified.

**Disposition:** Official applicant-list route only; **APPLICATION STATUS / NOT AN APPROVAL REGISTER / RESEARCH ONLY**.

## 6. National Agriculture Education Accreditation Council (NAEAC)

**Official routes:** [NAEAC official documents](https://www.naeac.org.pk/documents/guidelines_for_evaluators.pdf), [HEC accreditation-council map](https://www.hec.gov.pk/urdu/services/universities/QAA/Pages/Accreditation-Councils.aspx).

NAEAC is an HEC-established council responsible for accreditation of agricultural degree programmes. Its official evaluator guidance states that agriculture degree programmes across approximately 15 disciplines fall within its purview, with veterinary sciences excluded.

**V2 interpretation**
- NAEAC accreditation is agriculture-programme-specific, not institution-wide; veterinary education is handled by a separate council route.
- The evaluator-guidance PDF establishes process/scope, not the current approved programme list.
- A current, complete public accredited-programme register was not verified in this review.
- No source-specific reuse license was verified.

**Disposition:** Official council and scope evidence observed; current public programme register gap remains; **PARTIAL / RESEARCH ONLY**.

## 7. Cross-council status and privacy rules

1. Distinguish council mandate, application, NOC, recognition, accreditation, reaccreditation, expiry, under-process, suspension/stoppage and programme closure.
2. Preserve the exact object approved: provider, department/school, campus, programme, batch/intake or individual practitioner.
3. Store effective session/validity and source date; an undated directory cannot establish current approval.
4. Exclude personal names and direct contact numbers where not necessary for institution discovery.
5. All sources remain RESEARCH ONLY; no source-specific reuse license is established by this review.
