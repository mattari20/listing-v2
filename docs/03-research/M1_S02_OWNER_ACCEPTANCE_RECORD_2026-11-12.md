## Accepted taxonomy

The approved provisional discovery taxonomy covers:
1. School education providers, including public, private and international-curriculum schools.
2. Colleges and intermediate/degree colleges, including affiliated and constituent colleges.
3. Universities and degree-awarding institutions, including campuses and approved study centres.
4. Technical and vocational education and training providers, skills centres, apprenticeship providers and assessment centres.
5. Professional and specialized education providers and regulated programmes.
6. Special-education institutions and service/rehabilitation centres.
7. Non-formal, community, adult-literacy and alternative-learning providers.
8. Deeni Madaris as a distinct provider family.
9. Distance/open-learning providers, approved study centres and examination centres.

The taxonomy preserves separate relationships and concepts for provider, campus, programme, regulator, registration, recognition, accreditation, affiliation, examination/assessment body, qualification-awarding body and qualification equivalence. Ownership, gender access, delivery mode, education stage and geography are cross-cutting attributes, not mutually exclusive provider types.

## Limitations retained by this acceptance

- The taxonomy is a discovery classification, not a legal/regulatory classification and does not imply one national regulator for every provider family.
- The source matrix is not a complete national census or a production-verified dataset.
- Known jurisdiction, register-access, freshness, data-quality and source-coverage gaps remain as documented.
- No blanket education-directory reuse permission is established. Source-specific terms and permissions remain necessary.
- The only scoped reuse permission identified is for SED Balochistan dashboard headline metrics and district breakdowns with attribution to the School Education Department, Government of Balochistan, and an as-of date. It does not extend to school-level records, GIS/private/BEF records or other datasets.
- Personal, student, child, teacher, staff, CNIC, health, disability, therapy and other sensitive personal data remain excluded from the discovery model.
