## 1. AJK private-school source — announced, but access not verified

The official AJK E&SE page announces a registered-private-institution list dated 5 November 2025. The linked Google Drive PDF prompts sign-in in the public browser review; the new PEIRA portal redirects to a username/password login. No public unauthenticated register route was verified. Therefore the AJK private-school status is **partial official source lead — access unverified**, not a strong public register. Record contents, row count, district coverage, current status and reuse terms remain unverified. Do not bypass login or infer that the source is public merely because the announcement page is public.

## 2. ICT special education — partial official register identified

DGSE publishes four named education/rehabilitation centres in Islamabad; the Ministry of Human Rights lists 11 mixed service units under DGSE, including training, vocational rehabilitation, mobility, library/resource and press functions. DGSE is ICT-only, not a national directory. Treat the four centre providers separately from the other service units; do not classify all 11 as schools. Child admission identity, guardian CNIC, Form-B, disability/medical/therapy data and personal contacts are excluded.

## 4. Balochistan public/private/BEF map and legal registration update

SED's 2022 Private Educational Institutions Registration, Regulation and Promotion Act (Act XXVIII of 2022) expressly repeals the 2015 Act and assigns registering authorities by provider type: schools, colleges, technical institutes and teacher-training institutions. SED's August 2026 legal-framework webpage still describes the old 2015/2016 BPEIRRA/BEF arrangement; this is stale/inconsistent against the statute and should not be used as the current legal authority without clarification.

SED's 2026 GIS map provides separate Schools, Private, BEF and ALP layers. It identifies magenta pins as registered private schools, but does not publish a verified private-school count or a license for the underlying school-level pins. The public-school dashboard reports 15,368 schools dated 30 July 2026, while the map says 12,980 of 14,709 in its official-list denominator have GPS. The 659 difference between 15,368 and 14,709 must be reconciled; 1,729 records in the map denominator are not plotted for lack of valid GPS.

BEF's September 2026 consolidated list reports 2,795 community/fellowship/foundation-supported schools across 39 districts, of which 2,734 have GPS and 61 do not. Keep BEF schools distinct from government schools, registered private schools and ALP centres.

The SED dashboard's explicit open license remains scoped to headline metrics and district breakdowns with attribution and as-of date. It does not license GIS private-school pins, underlying school-level records, BEF records, student/teacher/payroll data or all department content.
