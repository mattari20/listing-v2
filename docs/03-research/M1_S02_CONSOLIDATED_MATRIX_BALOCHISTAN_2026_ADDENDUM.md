# M1-S02 — Consolidated Matrix Addendum: Balochistan 2026 Public-School and NFE Evidence

**Review date:** 2026-11-12  
**Supersedes:** The Balochistan public-school and NFE summary cells in the consolidated national coverage matrix where this addendum provides more recent evidence.  
**Status:** Research-only except for the narrowly scoped open license explicitly stated below.

## Updated public-school evidence

The official School Education Department, Government of Balochistan publishes a 2026 statistics dashboard:
- **15,368 public schools** from a BEMIS/DDO extract dated **30 July 2026**.
- **41 districts served**.
- The count includes school rows in the DDO-code list and excludes **329 administrative DDO rows**.
- A 2025–26 Annual School Census publication is dated 30 April 2026; a 2026–27 census collection circular is dated 10 June 2026.

The dashboard explicitly permits reuse of its **headline metrics and district breakdowns** with attribution to "School Education Department, Government of Balochistan" and citation of the metric's as-of date. This scoped license does not automatically extend to the underlying school-level record extract, student/teacher/payroll data or other department content. The 2023–24 EMIS statistics PDF is labelled DRAFT and its 15,270 count is historical, not the current 2026 aggregate.

Sources:
- https://www.sed.gob.pk/data-reports.html
- https://www.sed.gob.pk/documents.html
- https://www.sed.gob.pk/
- https://emis.gob.pk/Uploads/BalochistanEducationStatistics/Balochistan_Education_Statistics_2023-24.pdf

## Updated NFE evidence

The same SED dashboard reports **1,245 Non-Formal Education Centres** as of **28 February 2026**, sourced to the Directorate of Non-Formal Education. This is a current aggregate distinct from the organization-wise Balochistan EMIS NFE table. It is not a centre-level public register. Do not combine the two counts or infer unique operating providers without the underlying definitions and record-level list.

## Corrected coverage interpretation

- **Public schools:** strong current aggregate/district source with an underlying dated BEMIS/DDO extract; no public full school-level export or license for that extract was established.
- **NFE:** strong current aggregate (1,245, 28 Feb 2026) plus a separate organization-level aggregate table; centre-level inventory and deduplication remain unresolved.
- **Reuse:** dashboard headline metrics/district breakdowns = **OPEN LICENSE — SCOPED** with attribution and as-of date. Underlying school records and all other source families = **RESEARCH ONLY** until their own terms are verified.
- **Data quality:** keep the 329 excluded administrative DDO rows separate; keep school, administrative office, NFE centre, organization, programme and implementing partner as different record types.
