## 1. Completion rule

A research task is complete when its stated source-mapping, authority, status-semantics, access/rights/privacy assessment or consolidated-gap deliverable is documented with explicit limitations. A missing public register, unclear license, stale source, restricted access or unresolved count can be a completed research finding when the task is to identify and document that gap. Completion does not mean that all institution records have been harvested, independently verified or cleared for reuse.

No source is approved for ingestion by this audit. S03-C09 is an explicit owner-only acceptance gate and must not be self-approved by the assistant.

## 4. Principal limitations carried forward

1. Punjab PMIU's "free/open/real-time" wording is not a verified legal reuse license; PEPRIS uses owner CNIC/mobile search and has DEA-upload gaps.
2. Sindh's public institution checker and private DIRPIS are separate; no complete current public private-school register was verified.
3. KP public iEMIS is role-based; KPPSRA district results do not prove full province-wide completeness.
4. Balochistan's 15,368 public-school dashboard count differs from the 14,709 GIS denominator; the map has separate Private/BEF/ALP layers and school-level rights remain unverified.
5. ICT FDE's 432 is an aggregate; FDE, FGEI C/G and FBISE are distinct source families; PEIRA is an authenticated private-school route.
6. AJK E&SE's 6,019 aggregate lacks a clear as-of date; EMIS/PEIRA require login; the 2025 private-list file prompts sign-in; HED 178/180 totals conflict.
7. GB EMIS includes administrative office rows and a zero-valued summary route; current private-school and complete special-education registers remain unverified.
8. Several professional councils have stale/undated records, programme-specific statuses or incomplete current public registers; AHPC applicant status must not be treated as approval.
9. NFEMIS headline totals fluctuate; special-education pages include mixed service types and personal data that are excluded.
10. TVET counts and directory scopes differ across NAVTTC, provincial TEVTAs, boards, B-TEVTA and BSIS.
11. DGRE/Wafaq/IBCC/HEC roles are distinct; no general Madaris qualification equivalence or current nationwide Madaris register is inferred.
12. No blanket source reuse permission is established; no source-owner contacts or permission requests were made.

## 5. Privacy, rights and independence boundary

No legacy repository or legacy production data was used. No source scraping, bulk download, login/access bypass, source-owner contact, permission request, connector activation, application code, schema/database change, production access, migration, deployment, student/child/teacher/staff personal data, CNICs, B-Forms, payroll, health/disability/therapy data or sect/maslak data was collected.

The owner-approved Deeni Madaris requirement remains: included in the underlying provider scope, hidden from public listing by default at launch, with a configurable `Show Deeni Madaris` setting default OFF and controlled through an authorized administration/configuration path. Cross-category visibility for a separately registered TVET provider at a Madrasah location remains an M2 design question.

## 6. Owner acceptance gate

The owner must review the consolidated source register and this audit, then explicitly accept or request corrections. Acceptance would close S03-C09 and M1-S03 as a research baseline only. It would not authorize source ingestion/reuse (beyond the existing narrow SED aggregate permission), implementation, production access, M1-S04 or M2.
