# M1-S02 — Deep Jurisdiction, Special-Education and TVET Register Review

**Review date:** 2026-11-12  
**Scope:** Public official-source desk research only. No scraping, record harvesting, bulk downloads, external contacts, permission requests, connector activation or implementation.  
**Status:** Research-only evidence supplement. No directory is approved for reuse and this review does not close M1-S02.

## 1. Special-education source coverage — new findings

### Punjab: dedicated institute taxonomy and directory
The Punjab Special Education Department's Institutes page separates facilities by service category and publishes department-level counts: visually impaired (18), mentally challenged (14), hearing impaired (46), physically disabled (5), slow learners (36), special-education centres (172), degree colleges (7), teacher-training colleges (2) and an in-service training college. The categories may overlap and must not be added to create a unique-institution total. The department also publishes a district-address list of centres. The detailed list includes personal head mobile numbers and staff/institution emails; those personal mobile numbers and named staff fields are excluded from V2 research attributes. The page contains older terminology and category-specific admission/health information; V2 must use neutral, respectful service/facility categories and must not model or collect individual disability, diagnosis, learner or health data.

Sources:
- https://sed.punjab.gov.pk/institutes
- https://sed.punjab.gov.pk/centres
- https://sed.punjab.gov.pk/devolved_institutes

### Sindh: DEPD institution list
Sindh's Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities publishes a regional institution/centre list, including Karachi-region rehabilitation centres and vocational training/rehabilitation services. The page includes personal names and mobile contacts for regional directors, principals and in-charges. These fields are not required for institution discovery and are excluded from the V2 research model. The page is a useful official source lead but requires province-wide completeness, district coverage, current operational status and publication/update-date verification.

Source:
- https://depd.sindh.gov.pk/institutions/

### Gilgit-Baltistan: Directorate of Special Education
The GB Directorate of Special Education site describes its mandate and reports a Special Education Complex in Gilgit and Skardu, plus centres in Ghizer and Diamer. News/events separately refer to the opening of a centre at Gahkuch, Ghizer and a wheelchair-accessible bus for Chilas. This is a useful current authority and facility lead, but the site's own location summary and event references do not form a reconciled institution register. Confirm whether Chilas is a separate operating centre, whether Gahkuch is the Ghizer centre, the status of Skardu, and the current complete district inventory before asserting a count. The page also contains health/service descriptions; do not collect individual student or medical data.

Source:
- https://gbse.gbit.gov.pk/

### Balochistan: current development proposals, not a directory
A Balochistan Planning & Development Department notice dated 31 August 2026 reports that the PDWP examined proposals to establish Special Education Complexes at Turbat and Zhob. This demonstrates planned infrastructure, not that either complex is operational or an existing institute. A Pakistan Institute of Education inclusive-education publication describes four government-run institutions (Quetta, Sibi, Khuzdar and Turbat) but is historical/secondary context and must not be treated as a current directory. Current operating institute list, district coverage and operational status remain unverified.

Sources:
- https://pnd.balochistan.gov.pk/news/pdwp-examines-social-welfare-department-proposals-for-special-education-complexes
- https://pie.gov.pk/SiteImage/Publication/Inclusive.pdf

### AJK and ICT
AJK E&SE currently reports aggregate school/teacher/enrolment dashboard figures and advertises a registered-private-school list, but no complete current special-education institution register was verified in this pass. ICT's DGSE mandate lead remains a service/mandate page rather than a current facility directory. These are explicit gaps, not zero-coverage claims.

Source:
- https://ese.gok.pk/

## 2. TVET provider-directory findings

### KP-TEVTA: current aggregate and institution categories
KP-TEVTA's current homepage reports 33 total institutes and separates GCTs (degree-awarding colleges), GPIs (polytechnic/DAE), GTVCs (vocational centres) and employment exchanges. This is stronger current aggregate evidence than the older general sector estimates, but the 33 count is not a complete record-level directory in the reviewed page. Its 2026 news and procurement notices show current activity. A provider taxonomy must distinguish degree-awarding college, polytechnic, vocational centre and employment exchange; an employment exchange is not a training institute.

Source:
- https://kptevta.gov.pk/

### Sindh STEVTA: searchable official institute page
STEVTA publishes an official Institutes page with region and district filters and institution categories such as GIBCE, GPI, GVS, VTC, EE and GMI. This is a promising structured source for province-level institute coverage. The visible page is filter-driven and does not expose a complete count in the retrieved view; verify district/filter coverage, duplicate campuses, institute type definitions, active status, and export/access terms before claiming completeness.

Source:
- https://stevta.gos.pk/institutes.aspx

### Punjab TEVTA: institute data exists, but version/freshness and personal fields matter
Punjab TEVTA publishes an official vocational-institutes PDF with district/zone groupings, addresses, establishment year, coordinates and also direct contact details and head-of-institute names. The surfaced PDF is dated 2024 and therefore cannot be treated as a verified 2026 current register without a newer version or confirmation. The official RDAT page separately lists apprenticeship-training directorates; these should be classified as apprenticeship administration/training offices rather than assumed to be ordinary colleges. Personal staff names and direct personal numbers are excluded from the V2 research model.

Sources:
- https://tevta.gop.pk/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Voc_Institutes.pdf
- https://tevta.gop.pk/rdats/

### Balochistan B-TEVTA: public directory count requires scope reconciliation
The B-TEVTA directory currently displays 16 total institutes, while NAVTTC's sector overview cites 80+ B-TEVTA institutes. These numbers are not directly comparable until the directory's scope, provider types, inclusion criteria, update date and completeness are explained. Do not select either figure as a definitive total. Directory records also show spelling/administrative naming inconsistencies that require normalization only after source identity is verified.

Sources:
- https://registration.btevta.gob.pk/portal/directory
- https://navttc.gov.pk/tvet-sector/

### NAVTTC NAC-TVS directory: staging/sample data excluded
The surfaced NAC-TVS directory is hosted on a staging/test subdomain and explicitly labels its 26 entries as representative sample data. It contains geography inconsistencies (for example, a Gilgit-labelled provider placed under AJK in the staging result). This is not production evidence, must not be treated as a real accredited-provider register, and must not feed any count, coverage claim or V2 institution record. Only the production NAVTTC accreditation source and a verifiable official record should be considered for future evidence.

Source:
- https://stagging.navttc.gov.pk/accreditation/institutes

## 3. Cross-sector directory leads that must not be confused with recognition

### HEC National Digital Library institution directory
The HEC National Digital Library provides an institution directory with sector and province filters and access-profile context. It appears designed to identify institutions participating in subscribed digital-library resources. It is not, by itself, the HEC recognized-university list, campus recognition register, charter evidence or programme-accreditation register. Use only as a secondary institution-discovery lead, with its purpose clearly labelled.

Source:
- https://digitallibrary.edu.pk/institutions

### PASTIC university/institute database
PASTIC publishes a public/private universities and institutes database with a stated count of 278 institutions. The surfaced data includes malformed or inconsistent rows and mixed organization types. It is a secondary discovery/cross-check lead, not a regulator's recognized-institution register; do not use its aggregate as a national census without deduplication, record validation and scope/rights checks.

Source:
- https://pastic.gov.pk/database-universities.aspx

### HEC recognised universities
HEC's recognized-universities page provides filters for sector, chartering authority, discipline, province and city and a searchable institution listing. This remains the primary recognized-university discovery route for HEC status, but the recognized university list must be kept distinct from HEC recognized campuses, affiliated colleges, Pakistan Qualification Register entries and professional-council programme accreditation.

Source:
- https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/universities/Pages/recognised.aspx

## 4. Quality and rights controls

1. Never add category totals when facilities may appear in multiple service categories.
2. Distinguish an existing operational centre from a proposed development project, a service office, a rehabilitation facility, a training centre and an employment exchange.
3. Preserve the exact source status and effective date. Proposed, under construction, active, closed, devolved, disputed, applied-for, pending and accredited are different states.
4. Do not copy personal head/principal mobile numbers, staff names or personal email addresses into V2 institution profiles. Use institution-level official contact channels only after necessity and reuse rights are reviewed.
5. Do not collect student identities, profiles, disability categories linked to individuals, diagnoses, health/therapy records or learner-level data.
6. Public accessibility is not permission for automated collection, storage, republication or commercial use. No source-specific license or written permission was established in this review.
7. Directory aggregate counts are source claims, not verified national totals. Record date, scope, source owner and reconciliation state before any quantitative use.
