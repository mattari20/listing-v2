# M1-S03 — TVET Authorities, QABs and Provider Directory Source Review

**Review date:** 2026-11-12  
**Scope:** NAVTTC/QAB framework, provincial TEVTA authorities and official institute-directory routes, technical boards and cross-listed Deeni Madaris vocational training.  
**Status:** Supplemental research evidence; M1-S03 remains in progress.

## 1. NAVTTC — QAB and certification framework

**Official route:** [Accreditation & Certification](https://navttc.gov.pk/wing/accreditation-and-certification/) and [Institute Affiliation](https://navttc.gov.pk/affiliation/).

NAVTTC's public Accreditation & Certification page identifies registered Qualification Awarding Bodies (QABs) authorized to conduct assessments under its framework. The current page lists PBTE, SBTE, KP-BT&CE, TTB Punjab, TTB Sindh, TTB KPK, TTB Balochistan, AJKTTB, NAVTTC Direct Assessment, PVTC, NBTPE, NUMS, FBISE and NTB (14 listed bodies).

NAVTTC's affiliation explanation states that an institute first registers with NAVTTC and then affiliates with an approved QAB relevant to its province/trade. The QAB conducts examinations, assessment and certification; it is not necessarily the training provider.

**V2 distinctions**
- NAVTTC registration, QAB affiliation, NAC-TVS accreditation, training delivery, assessment and certification are separate relationships/statuses.
- A QAB list is not a list of all training institutes.
- The NAVTTC production page and older/staging pages show different QAB list counts/composition. Use the production official page as the current observed route and retain the page-check date; do not combine counts across production, staging and test sites.
- No source-specific reuse license was verified.

**Disposition:** Official QAB route observed; **RESEARCH ONLY**.

## 2. Punjab TEVTA, PSDA, TTB Punjab and vocational-provider sources

**Official routes:** [Punjab TEVTA vocational institutes](https://tevta.gop.pk/list-of-vocational-institutes/), [TEVTA institute locator](https://portal.tevta.gop.pk/), [Punjab government skills-development authority map](https://icid.punjab.gov.pk/skills-development), [TTB Punjab affiliation](https://ttbp.edu.pk/institutional-affiliation/), [TTB Punjab non-TEVTA institutes](https://ttbp.edu.pk/non-tevta-institutes/), [TTB Punjab TEVTA institutes — Central Zone](https://ttbp.edu.pk/tevta-institutes-central-zone/), [TTB Punjab Deeni Madaris/Jails](https://ttbp.edu.pk/other-tevta-institutes-jails-deeni-madaras/), [TEVTA Deeni Madaris](https://tevta.gop.pk/deeni-madarsa/).

The Punjab Industries, Commerce, Investment and Skills Development Department identifies TEVTA as a special institution under its administrative control and describes PSDA as the apex regulator for the skills sector in Punjab. The department's page cites 400+ TEVTA institutes and 90,000 annual training capacity as aggregate claims, not a reconciled live register.

Punjab TEVTA provides a vocational-institute list and links to an institute locator/admission portal. TTB Punjab separately publishes zone-specific TEVTA institute lists, non-TEVTA institutes and a combined "Jails & Deeni Madaras" training-centre list. TTB describes institutional affiliation as a formal link to a QAB for examination/certification and states that it affiliates both public and private technical/vocational institutes.

**Deeni Madaris overlap finding**
- TTB Punjab has a separate public directory titled "Other TEVTA Institutes (Jails & Deeni Madaras)" with 13 Deeni Madaris centre entries followed by jail training centres.
- Punjab TEVTA also publishes a Deeni Madaris list with attached TEVTA institutes and vocational trades.
- These records describe vocational training/assessment relationships involving some Madaris. They do not convert every Deeni Madrasa into a TEVTA institute or imply general academic recognition.
- Keep the Deeni Madrasa provider identity distinct from the attached vocational institute, training programme, QAB and assessment relationship. Do not duplicate one provider as several institutions without evidence of separate legal/provider identities.
- This overlap does not change owner decision V2-DEC-006: Deeni Madaris public listing visibility remains OFF by default. Whether a separately registered vocational sub-provider operating at the same address may be independently visible when the Madaris category is hidden is an M2 product-design question; do not infer the answer from these directories.

**Data quality/privacy**
- TTB zone lists and the vocational-institute list are different source families with potentially overlapping records; deduplication by centre code, provider identity, location and affiliation is required.
- TEVTA's 2024 PDF and older zone lists are not automatically current 2026 registers.
- TTB affiliation is not itself proof of current programme approval or student admission availability.
- Some pages include centre codes and public institute contacts; do not copy individual staff/owner personal data.
- No source-specific reuse license was verified.

**Disposition:** Official authority and directory routes observed; **RESEARCH ONLY**.

## 3. Sindh STEVTA

**Official route:** [STEVTA Institutes](https://stevta.gos.pk/institutes.aspx).

STEVTA's official site provides a public institute page with region and district filters. Visible records include institute-type abbreviations such as GIBCE, GPI, GVS, VTC, EE and GMI, with location information. The page uses a paginated/filter-driven presentation and does not expose a dataset-wide as-of date or public reuse license.

**V2 interpretation**
- Preserve the provider type and region/district; do not infer full province-wide coverage from the first visible page.
- Verify abbreviations, operational status and the complete filtered result scope before using a record.
- Keep STEVTA as training authority/provider network distinct from SBTE as a QAB/examination/awarding body.

**Disposition:** Public filtered institute route observed; **RESEARCH ONLY**.

## 4. Khyber Pakhtunkhwa TEVTA

**Official routes:** [Vocational trades/institutes](https://kptevta.gov.pk/vocational-trades/) and [DAE/B.Tech institute/programme list](https://kptevta.gov.pk/dae-btech/).

KPTEVTA publishes vocational institute/trade tables and DAE institute/programme tables. The DAE page names GCT/GPI institutions and lists their trades; the vocational page names GTVC/GATTC sites and course/trade options.

**V2 interpretation**
- These pages combine institute and course rows; do not count each course as an institution.
- Course durations and qualifications differ (e.g. six-month CBT&A and traditional two-year trade routes); preserve programme/trade and delivery mode.
- The pages do not show a clear dataset-wide as-of date or complete private-provider coverage.
- Keep KP Board of Technical & Commerce Education and Trade Testing Board as QAB/examination bodies separate from KPTEVTA training providers.

**Disposition:** Official institute/trade source route observed; **RESEARCH ONLY**.

## 5. Balochistan B-TEVTA and Skills Information System (BSIS)

**Official routes:** [B-TEVTA public directory](https://registration.btevta.gob.pk/portal/directory), [B-TEVTA portal](https://registration.btevta.gob.pk/), [BSIS TVET Providers](https://bsis.btevta.gob.pk/institute-list).

The B-TEVTA public directory labels itself an official accredited-provider registry and displays 16 total institutes and 41 active districts; the home page has displayed a different headline of six approved institutions. The directory's first page shows only a subset of the stated 16 entries. Treat the six-versus-16 display as an unresolved route/scope discrepancy, not a reconciled total.

BSIS presents a wider TVET-provider inventory and district filters. Its page displays 413 total institutes but gender breakdown values of 27 male, 11 female and 74 co-educational institutes (112 combined). The relationship between the 413 total and the 112 gender figures is not explained on the page. BSIS also describes a programme-supported TVET data ecosystem involving government and private stakeholders; it should not be assumed to be identical to the B-TEVTA accredited registry.

**V2 interpretation**
- B-TEVTA accredited directory, B-TEVTA registration workflow and BSIS broader TVET provider inventory are separate sources with distinct scopes.
- Do not treat 6, 16 or 413 as one authoritative Balochistan TVET count.
- Preserve source, registry type, accreditation/registration state, district and as-of date; validate duplicate and inactive entries.
- No source-specific reuse license was verified.

**Disposition:** Public source routes observed; count/scope discrepancies unresolved; **RESEARCH ONLY**.

## 6. Cross-source QAB relationship model

For each TVET provider, preserve separate links to:
1. Provider/institution identity.
2. Training location/campus or delivery site.
3. Programme/trade, duration and NVQF/qualification level.
4. Provider registration and regulator.
5. QAB affiliation and effective period.
6. Assessment body and assessment route.
7. Qualification/certificate issuer.
8. Accreditation status, scope and validity.
9. Public visibility category and visibility setting.

A provider can deliver training while another body examines or certifies the qualification. The provider, QAB, trade test board, TEVTA, PSDA, NAVTTC and programme/accreditation status must not be collapsed into one "recognized" field.
