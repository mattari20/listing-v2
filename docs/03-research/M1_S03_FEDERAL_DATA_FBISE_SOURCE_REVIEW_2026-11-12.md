# M1-S03 — Federal Education Statistics, NEMIS and FBISE Source Review

**Review date:** 2026-11-12  
**Scope:** Pakistan Institute of Education (PIE), Pakistan Education Statistics, National EMIS and FBISE affiliated-institution source routes.  
**Status:** Supplemental research evidence; M1-S03 remains in progress.

## 1. Pakistan Institute of Education (PIE) and NEMIS mandate

**Official routes:** [PIE mandate/aims](https://pie.gov.pk/Detail/Nzg0OGRkY2MtM2NhOC00Njc0LTk0MGMtYjg2NGI1M2IzNTE2), [NEMIS overview](https://pie.gov.pk/Detail/NTIxYzhlMWYtZTU2YS00N2E4LWEzMDQtZjEwMjUwNDhjZmQ3), [data compilation](https://pie.gov.pk/Detail/MDUzZWJmNzAtZDU2YS00MWJhLWE5MTktMmE3MTIzMzkyN2U3), [EMIS overview](https://pie.gov.pk/Detail/MTIxZWQ4YjUtMjA4ZS00MTUxLWE5ZGItMWRjM2NkOWMyNGRl).

PIE states that it was approved as an executive/attached department of MoFEPT in November 2021 and inaugurated in March 2022. Its mandate includes education data collection, consolidation, dissemination, assessment, research and technical support. NEMIS coordinates provincial/regional EMIS data; district EMIS cells conduct annual school censuses and send data through provincial/regional EMIS units for national consolidation.

PIE identifies the provincial/regional EMIS partners as Punjab, Sindh, KP, Balochistan, AJK, GB and ICT/FDE. This supports a federated data-custody model rather than assuming PIE owns one live record-level national school database.

## 2. Pakistan Education Statistics 2024 — methodology and coverage caveats

**Official publication:** [Pakistan Education Statistics 2024](https://www.pie.gov.pk/SiteImage/Publication/PES%202024.pdf).

The 2024 report provides important source-methodology distinctions:
- Non-formal education statistics for 2023–24 are sourced from NF-EMIS, which PIE says contains public and private NFE centres nationwide.
- Public intermediate/degree-college statistics are provided by the relevant provincial/regional higher-education departments.
- The report states that provincial/regional departments do not maintain private-college databases except in Punjab and KP. This is a material national private-college coverage gap and means a national aggregate may combine direct data with incomplete or unavailable private-college source data.
- The source, year and coverage method must be retained for each indicator. A national aggregate should not be presented as a complete current private-college register.

## 3. Deeni Madaris statistics — Wafaq reporting limitation

**Historical official publication:** [Pakistan Education Statistics 2021–22](https://www.pie.gov.pk/SiteImage/Publication/PES%20Report%202021-22.pdf).

The 2021–22 report says Deeni Madaris data are obtained annually from Wafaqs notified by MoFEPT using a prescribed template. It also states that actual data for 2021–22 were supplied by some Wafaqs while data for remaining Wafaqs were repeated. The report notes that Wafaq-level computerized systems and portals cover Madaris registered with their respective Wafaqs.

**V2 implications**
- This is historical aggregate methodology, not a current national provider register.
- Wafaq registration coverage is not proof of DGRE registration or HEC/IBCC qualification equivalence.
- Data repeated for non-reporting Wafaqs must not be treated as current-year verified records.
- The source does not establish complete coverage of all Deeni Madaris or their individual campuses.
- Preserve separate provider-registration, Wafaq/QAB relationship and qualification-equivalence evidence.
- This evidence supports retaining Deeni Madaris as a distinct research family while maintaining the owner's default-hidden public visibility decision. It does not authorize ingestion or reuse.

## 4. Federal Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education (FBISE)

**Official route:** [FBISE affiliated institutions](https://portal.fbise.edu.pk/fbise-conduct/ref-inst).

The public route displays an affiliated-institution table with institution code, name and level; the page displayed 3,315 institutions in the reviewed snapshot. The sample includes schools and colleges across multiple provinces/territories and some overseas institutions. It also contains mixed provider types, including health/technical institutions and Madaris.

**V2 interpretation**
- FBISE affiliation means examination-board affiliation for SSC/HSSC scope; it is not equivalent to institutional registration, HEC recognition, professional accreditation or a general operating license.
- The directory is not limited to Islamabad/ICT; it includes institutions in other provinces, territories and overseas locations.
- The source needs duplicate/campus normalization and country/jurisdiction parsing before any coverage count is used.
- The displayed 3,315 is a time-sensitive snapshot; no dataset-wide as-of date or reuse license was verified.
- A Madrasah listed as an FBISE-affiliated examination provider remains a separate Deeni Madaris provider family for V2 visibility purposes; board affiliation does not override the owner's default-hidden listing rule.

**Disposition:** Official board-affiliation route observed; **RESEARCH ONLY**.
