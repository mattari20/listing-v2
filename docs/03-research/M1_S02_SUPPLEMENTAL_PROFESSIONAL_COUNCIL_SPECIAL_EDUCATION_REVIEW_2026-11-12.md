# M1-S02 — Supplemental Professional-Council and Special-Education Review

**Review date:** 2026-11-12  
**Scope:** Independent desk research of public official pages. Documentation only; no collection, scraping, source-owner contact, permission requests or implementation.  
**Status:** Research evidence only. It does not approve a source for reuse or close M1-S02.

## 1. Professional and accreditation council findings

### Pakistan Council for Architects and Town Planners (PCATP)
PCATP's 2025 annual report describes programme-level status categories and reports 39 architectural institutions listed, of which 26 programmes were accredited, two new programmes awaited NOC decisions, one institution's accreditation was on hold, one accreditation had been revoked and three B.Arch programmes had closed. It also reports five town-planning institutions with all five programmes accredited. The report lists three B.Arch programmes under review. A separate PCATP PDF dated 22 September 2025 is explicitly a list of institutions that **applied for NOC** to offer B.Arch; application must not be represented as NOC approval or accreditation. The current record-level annexure, campus/intake scope and effective dates must be checked before any individual programme status is asserted.

Sources:
- https://pcatp.org.pk/document/Annual%20Report%202025.pdf
- https://pcatp.org.pk/document/Accreditation%20Status%20of%20Institutions%20by%20Province%2022Sep25.pdf

### National Council for Tibb (NCT)
NCT publishes separate pages for recognized FTJ institutions and recognized BEMS departments. The FTJ page includes a visibly marked disputed institution and numerous direct personal phone numbers/emails; this requires caution and personal-contact exclusion. The BEMS page lists departments at named universities. NCT also publishes registered-tabib information, which is an individual professional-registration dataset and is outside the institution-discovery scope. Do not conflate a college listing, programme recognition, a registered practitioner or a disputed entry.

Sources:
- https://nct.gov.pk/NCT-Educations/Recognized-Institutions--%28FTJ%29
- https://nct.gov.pk/NCT-Educations/Recognized-BEMS-Departments
- https://nct.gov.pk/Registered-Tabibs-List.html

### Allied Health Professionals Council (AHPC)
The Allied Health Professionals Council Act, 2022 provides that the Council should maintain a public register of institutions and qualifications granted recognition. However, the public PDF located in this review is titled **List of Institutions Applied for Recognition / Accreditation with AHPC** (dated 16 December 2025). An application list is not proof of recognition or accreditation. It must not be presented as an approved-institute register; the current public register of granted recognitions and qualifications remains to be verified.

Sources:
- https://ahpc.org.pk/assets/final-list-of-institutes-16-12-25.pdf
- https://www.ahpc.org.pk/assets/allied-health-professionals-council-act-20212.pdf

### National Technology Council (NTC)
NTC publishes a list of B.E.Tech/BSc Technology programmes under zero and interim phases, and a separate accreditation-visit detail page with institution, programme and intake-batch information. These are useful programme-level evidence routes but zero/interim phase and a completed visit must not be interpreted as final accreditation. The visit page contains historical intake batches; current status and valid intake must be checked at programme/campus/batch level. NTC states that each campus of a multi-campus institution is considered separately in evaluation/accreditation.

Sources:
- https://www.ntc-hec.org.pk/etpuz.php
- https://www.ntc-hec.org.pk/aetp.php
- https://www.ntc-hec.org.pk/faq.php

### National Agricultural Education Accreditation Council (NAEAC)
The HEC accreditation-council map identifies NAEAC, but this review did not verify a complete current public programme/institution register with reliable validity and status semantics. Keep NAEAC as an identified authority with a public-register verification gap; do not infer approvals from an institution's own marketing claims.

Source:
- https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/universities/qaa/pages/accreditation-councils.aspx

## 2. Special-education coverage findings

### Islamabad Capital Territory
The Ministry of Human Rights describes the Directorate General of Special Education (DGSE) as functioning at ICT level following the 18th Amendment, with special-education, training and rehabilitation services. This establishes an official ICT-level authority/source lead, but the page reviewed is a mandate/services description, not a current institution register. A current institute list, operating status, location coverage and terms remain to be verified.

Source:
- https://www.mohr.gov.pk/Detail/MzYzY2VkMzctMGZjYi00YjRhLWFiNDktMTA3ZDdjNWJiMTM3

### Khyber Pakhtunkhwa
A KP government-hosted parliamentary attachment lists institutions by district for the 2020–21 student-support year. It is historical evidence only and cannot establish current operating status or current coverage. The KP 2025 parity report provides high-level district/gender discussion, but not a validated current institution register.

Sources:
- https://www.pakp.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/14398.pdf
- https://cmkp.gov.pk/ParityReport2025.pdf

### Balochistan, AJK and Gilgit-Baltistan
No current, complete official special-education institution register for these jurisdictions was verified in this review. Historical national/provincial summaries are not accepted as current directory evidence.

## 3. Data-use and privacy cautions

- A list of institutions that applied for recognition/NOC is not a list of approved institutions.
- Zero/interim phase, under review, pending decision, on hold, revoked, closed and disputed are distinct states; preserve source wording and effective dates.
- NCT and special-education pages expose direct staff/principal contact details. Do not copy or republish personal phone numbers, personal emails or staff names as institution attributes.
- Registered practitioners, student profiles, staff profiles and sensitive health/disability information are outside the V2 institution-discovery scope.
- Public page visibility does not establish a license or written permission for collection, republication or commercial use. All sources remain RESEARCH ONLY.

## 4. Acceptance implications

The new evidence improves professional-council coverage and clarifies status semantics, particularly for PCATP, NCT, AHPC and NTC. It does not complete record-level verification, prove current multi-jurisdiction special-education coverage, establish reuse rights or authorize ingestion.
