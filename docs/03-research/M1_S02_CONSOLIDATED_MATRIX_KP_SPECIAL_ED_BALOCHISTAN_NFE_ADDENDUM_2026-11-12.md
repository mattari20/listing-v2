## Updated evidence

### KP special education — partial official source, not a complete register
The KP Zakat, Ushr, Social Welfare, Special Education & Women Empowerment Department publishes a page with 15 named centre/provider entries and an education-level field. One entry, Govt School for MR & PH Malakand, is explicitly marked **NON FUNCTIONAL**. The page has no clear update date, and some rows mix centre names and service/organization names. Treat this as a partial official source lead, with other records marked status-not-stated until verified. The 2026 government activity reports corroborate existing service activity in Lower Chitral and Peshawar; proposed new complexes in Khyber/Bajaur remain proposals, not operating centres.

Sources:
- https://www.swkpk.gov.pk/mentally-retarded-students.php
- https://dgipr.kp.gov.pk/chief-ministers-adviser-malak-liaqat-ali-khan-conducts-detailed-visit-to-lower-chitral-reviews-social-welfare-services/
- https://dgipr.kp.gov.pk/social-welfare-7-9-26/
- https://dgipr.kp.gov.pk/social-welfare-4-9-26/

Do not use the enrolment aggregates on the department page in the V2 institution directory. Exclude learner identity, disability/diagnosis, health/therapy and staff personal data.

### Balochistan NFE — organization-level aggregates, not centre inventory
Balochistan EMIS has a dedicated NFE page with organization-wise centre, teacher and learner aggregate columns for 12 named organizations/programmes, including the Directorate of Literacy & NFE, BECS, NCHD, BRSP, NRSP and partner programmes. The retrieved page has no visible reporting date and does not expose a record-level list of every centre. Some figures/columns are unusual or repeated, and implementing partners/programmes must not be mistaken for unique provider organizations. Do not add the displayed figures into a verified unique centre total.

Source:
- https://emis.gob.pk/NF/NonFormalHome.aspx
